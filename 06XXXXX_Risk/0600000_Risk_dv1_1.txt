============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Domain Document for AA=06 Risk. It
defines the entire Risk domain: its purpose, scope,
governing principles, hard rules, and the complete
structure of all controlled documents within it.

The Risk domain governs every rule, formula, threshold,
and decision that controls how much capital TothBot V2
commits to any trade or set of trades, when TothBot pauses
or halts due to adverse performance, and how position
concentration and capital exposure are bounded at all times.

Nothing that touches capital allocation, drawdown, or
position exposure is unspecified. Nothing is assumed.
Everything is explicitly defined in this domain or in the
canonical governance sources cited herein (TB00000 v2_49
+ 0500000 dv1_80).

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

The Risk domain owns:

  - Position sizing framework: per-module trading wallets
    (wallet_balance_long_usd / wallet_balance_short_usd
    per TB00000 v2_49 §9.4), the R:R-driven sizing curve
    above the sacred 1:1.5 R:R floor (TB00000 §9.10), the
    per_trade_ceiling_pct concentration cap, and the Gate 8
    Position Sizer computation
  - Drawdown monitoring: per-module portfolio_baseline
    definition (per TB00000 v2_49 §9.7 PER-WALLET DRAWDOWN
    HALTS), drawdown formula, evaluation triggers, and the
    two per-wallet circuit breaker thresholds (session pause
    and full halt) evaluated independently per module
  - Concentration rules: the per_trade_ceiling_pct hard cap
    per single trade (TB00000 §9.17 — concurrent-position
    count emerges from wallet_balance / per_trade_size_usd
    arithmetic), asyncio.BoundedSemaphore enforcement
    mechanism
  - Exposure rules: Pending Order Registry, available_USD
    computation, USD sufficiency check at Gate 7
  - Safety parameter framework: the complete registry of
    all CIATS-owned risk parameters lives in TB00000 v2_49
    §9.17 (canonical) and is mirrored in 0500000 dv1_80 §9
  - The one hardcoded constraint: net 1:1.5 R:R MINIMUM
    FLOOR. This constraint is NOT CIATS-owned. It never
    changes. It is the sole parameter sacred from CIATS
    authority. CIATS owns the sizing curve ABOVE the floor.

The Risk domain does NOT own:

  - Trading strategy signals or entry criteria (AA=07)
  - Order dispatch and execution mechanics (AA=08)
  - Exchange wire protocol (AA=04)
  - CIATS improvement cycle procedures (AA=02)
  - System component architecture (AA=05)
  - The emergSL stop-market order itself (AR-008)
    Risk defines the emergSL price. Exchange Interface
    defines the order type. WS Manager places the order.

The boundary is financial exposure. The Risk domain owns
every rule that governs how much money is at risk and under
what conditions TothBot stops committing more.

============================================================
3. GOVERNING PRINCIPLES
============================================================

3.1 Loss Prevention Takes Priority Over Profit Generation

This is the most critical mission function. Every risk
rule is designed first to prevent loss, second to enable
profit. When these objectives conflict, loss prevention
wins. This is not negotiable. It is encoded in the
1:1.5 R:R minimum floor, the per-wallet drawdown
thresholds, and the per_trade_ceiling_pct cap.

3.2 The Sacred Constraint — Net 1:1.5 R:R Minimum Floor

Net 1:1.5 risk-to-reward is the MINIMUM expected R:R on
every trade. It is the only hardcoded constraint in
TothBot V2. CIATS does not own the floor. Bill does not
change it. It never changes. Every trade must target at
minimum 1.5 units of net gain for every 1 unit of net
risk, after fees. This is the floor below which no trade
may be placed.

CIATS owns the sizing curve ABOVE the floor — trades with
expected R:R above 1:1.5 may receive larger per-trade
allocations than baseline, bounded above by
per_trade_ceiling_pct (TB00000 v2_49 §9.10 + §9.17).

Sacred constraint canonical sources:
  TB00000 v2_49 §9.10 (rule statement + acceptance rule A1).
  0500000 dv1_80 §4 AR-011 (architectural reasoning
    canonical source).
  0500000 dv1_80 rule:Sacred_R_R_1_to_1_5 (governs
    registry entry).
  0500000 dv1_80 D2 R2 G7+G8 risk-gate architecture.

3.3 All Parameters Are CIATS-Owned Starting Values

Every risk parameter except net 1:1.5 R:R minimum floor
is a starting value subject to data-driven optimization
by CIATS. No parameter is sacred. No parameter is
permanent. CIATS replaces every starting value with data
after the 200-trade floor is reached. PDCA governs all
changes. Parameter philosophy canonical source: TB00000
v2_49 §9.16. Parameter values canonical source: TB00000
v2_49 §9.17 + 0500000 dv1_80 §9 Complete CIATS Parameter
Registry mirror.

3.4 Cash Is the Sizing Basis — MTM Is the Drawdown Basis

Position sizing uses per-module trading wallet cash
balance only. Drawdown monitoring uses current per-module
wallet value including mark-to-market of open positions
held by that module. These are two separate computations
with two different wallet-value definitions. They must
never be conflated.

  Sizing:   wallet_balance (cash only per module)
  Drawdown: current_wallet_value = cash + sum(bid * qty)
            evaluated per module independently

3.5 No Parameter Change Without the 200-Trade Floor

Statistical inference is not valid below 200 closed trades.
CIATS monitoring is active below 200 trades (EWMA at 50
candle evals). CIATS parameter changes are prohibited
below 200 trades. The Proposal Engine does not activate
until the 200-trade floor is reached. Per-module CIATS
framework applies (TB00000 v2_49 §9.6) — each module's
200-trade threshold is independent.

3.6 The System Does Not Auto-Close on Halt

A full halt (per-wallet full_halt_drawdown_pct breach,
10% starting per TB00000 v2_49 §9.17) blocks all new
entries on that module's wallet and alerts Bill. It does
NOT auto-close open positions. Bill decides whether and
how to close open positions after a halt. Existing
resting TP and emergSL protection remains active on all
open positions through and after a halt.

============================================================
4. RISK ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
============================================================

The Risk domain operates at two points in the trading
pipeline and one continuous monitoring loop:

------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Gate 7 — Risk Guard (Pipeline Entry Check)
------------------------------------------------------------

Fires on every 5-minute OHLC candle close event, after
the Selection Controller passes a candidate pair.

Gate 7 checks (in order):
  (1) Drawdown check: is per-wallet drawdown_pct below
      both thresholds?
      If >= full_halt_drawdown_pct (10% starting):
        HALT this module's wallet. Block all entries.
      If >= session_pause_drawdown_pct (5% starting):
        PAUSE this module's wallet. Block entries.
      Resumes automatically when drawdown recovers below
      the session-pause threshold.
      Full halt requires manual operator action to resume.
      Per-wallet evaluation: a Long_Module halt does NOT
      halt Short_Module, and vice versa.
  (2) Concentration check: is per_trade_ceiling_pct
      respected?
      asyncio.BoundedSemaphore enforces concurrent-
      position bookkeeping. Concurrent-position count is
      not artificially capped; it emerges from
      wallet_balance / per_trade_size_usd arithmetic per
      TB00000 v2_49 §9.17 RETIRED rationale.
  (3) Exposure check: is available_USD >= per_trade_USD?
      available_USD = wallet_balance
                      - sum(pending_orders.values())
      Pending Order Registry tracks all unconfirmed entries.

Gate 7 NEVER blocks on size calculation. If Gate 7 passes,
Gate 8 fires unconditionally. Canonical architecture
source: 0500000 dv1_80 Image 8 + D2 R2 governs.

------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Gate 8 — Position Sizer (Size Calculation)
------------------------------------------------------------

Fires immediately after Gate 7 pass. Never blocks.
Computes three values in Decimal arithmetic:

  order_qty:    how much base currency to buy
  tp_price:     resting TP limit price (1:1.5 R:R net
                minimum; CIATS sizing curve above the
                floor)
  emergSL_price: emergency stop-market price (ATR x
                emergency_sl_mult, 3.0x starting)

All quantities and prices computed using Python Decimal.
All rounding uses Decimal.quantize() only. float
arithmetic and math.floor/ceil are PROHIBITED (BP-DEC-001
through BP-DEC-008 in 1011001). Canonical architecture
source: 0500000 dv1_80 Image 9 + D2 R2 governs.

------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Continuous Drawdown Monitor (Between Pipeline Events)
------------------------------------------------------------

Drawdown is NOT evaluated only at Gate 7. It is evaluated
continuously on every bbo ticker event for any pair with
an open position. This ensures a rapid market move between
pipeline evaluations triggers the halt in real time, not
at the next candle close. Evaluation is per-module-wallet
independent.

Drawdown formula (per module):
  drawdown_pct = max(0,
      (wallet_baseline - current_wallet_value)
      / wallet_baseline)

max(..., 0): wallet gains are not counted as negative
drawdown. The circuit breaker is one-directional.
Canonical architecture source: 0500000 dv1_80 §4
AR-052 + AR-048 + D5 architecture.

5. POSITION SIZING RULES SUMMARY
============================================================

Per-module wallet funding + R:R-driven sizing curve
framework. Canonical parameter values: TB00000 v2_49
§9.17 (canonical CIATS Parameter Registry) + 0500000
dv1_80 §9 (mirror registry).

Framework summary:
  - Each trading module (Long_Module, Short_Module,
    future modules) operates against its own trading
    wallet funded by Bill with affordable-loss capital
    per TB00000 v2_49 §9.4.
  - Per-trade size is a CIATS-owned function of expected
    R:R, starting at the Kraken per-pair minimum and
    evolving from the 200-trade inference threshold
    forward per TB00000 v2_49 §9.17 sizing-curve entry.
  - Per-trade allocation is bounded above by
    per_trade_ceiling_pct (25% of wallet starting,
    CIATS-adjustable per TB00000 v2_49 §9.17).
  - The 1:1.5 R:R minimum floor is enforced at signal
    acceptance per TB00000 v2_49 §9.10 acceptance rule
    A1. CIATS may never propose, size, or accept a trade
    below the floor.

Retired starting values (formerly enumerated in this
section per dv1_0; retired at TB00000 v2_29):
  tradeable_pct = 0.50 (50% of portfolio_USD) - REPLACED
    by operator-controlled per-module wallet funding per
    TB00000 v2_49 §9.4.
  per_trade_pct = 0.05 (5% of tradeable_USD) - REPLACED
    by per_trade_size_usd starting at Kraken per-pair
    minimum and evolving as CIATS-owned function of
    expected R:R per TB00000 v2_49 §9.17.
  max_concurrent = 20 positions - REPLACED by emergent
    arithmetic (wallet_balance / per_trade_size_usd); no
    artificial concurrent-position ceiling.
  Hardcoded Half-Kelly transition at 200 trades -
    REPLACED. Half-Kelly remains a candidate sizing
    methodology CIATS may adopt as the underlying
    sizing-curve form; it is no longer a hardcoded
    transition rule.

Full sizing-curve canonical source: TB00000 v2_49 §9.10
+ §9.17 sizing-curve subsection.

6. DRAWDOWN AND HALT RULES SUMMARY
============================================================

Per-module wallet drawdown framework. Drawdown thresholds
are evaluated per-module-wallet independently per TB00000
v2_49 §9.7.

Baseline definition (per module):
  wallet_baseline = module wallet_balance captured ONCE
  at startup (after snap_orders processing in startup
  sequence). NEVER updated during operation. NEVER reset
  on mid-session reconnect. Resets only on full TothBot
  process restart.

Current wallet value definition (for drawdown monitoring,
per module):
  current_wallet_value = wallet_balance
      + sum(bid_price[sym] * position_qty[sym]
            for sym in position_mirror
            where module owns position)
  Uses bid price (realizable exit value). Conservative.

Per-wallet circuit breakers (TB00000 v2_49 §9.17
starting values):
  session_pause_drawdown_pct (5% starting):
    Block all new entries for this module's wallet.
    Alert Bill.
    Existing positions monitored normally.
    Resumes automatically when this module's drawdown
    recovers below the session-pause threshold.
    Other modules' wallets unaffected.

  full_halt_drawdown_pct (10% starting):
    HALT this module's wallet. Block all new entries on
    this module. Alert Bill immediately.
    Does NOT auto-close positions. Bill decides.
    Requires manual operator action to resume.
    Other modules' wallets unaffected.

Both thresholds are CIATS-owned per-module starting
values per TB00000 v2_49 §9.7 + §9.17. CIATS may
propose adjustments from the per-module 200-trade
inference threshold forward.

============================================================
7. CONCENTRATION AND EXPOSURE RULES SUMMARY
============================================================

Per-trade concentration cap framework. Replaces the
retired max_concurrent ceiling (TB00000 v2_29 retirement;
TB00000 v2_49 §9.17 RETIRED rationale: concurrent-position
count emerges from wallet_balance / per_trade_size_usd
arithmetic; no artificial ceiling).

Per-trade ceiling:
  per_trade_ceiling_pct: 25% of module wallet starting
  (CIATS-owned, CIATS-adjustable per TB00000 v2_49
  §9.17). Hard concentration ceiling per single trade.
  Prevents a wrong R:R estimate at high allocation from
  wiping the wallet in one trade.

Concurrent-position bookkeeping:
  asyncio.BoundedSemaphore tracks concurrent positions
  per module for accounting integrity. BoundedSemaphore
  raises ValueError on over-release. ValueError =
  critical bug. Log and alert immediately. The semaphore
  is NOT a hard concurrent-position cap; it is a
  bookkeeping enforcement mechanism.

Pending Order Registry:
  pending_orders: dict[str, Decimal]
    maps cl_ord_id -> estimated_USD_cost
  Populated on entry dispatch:
    estimated_cost = order_qty * entry_limit_price
                     * (1 + FEE_MAKER_PCT)
  Cleared on: exec_type=filled, expired, or canceled.
  Held (not cleared) on: exec_type=trade (partial fill
  — order still open, position partially entered).
  Reconciled at startup and on every mid-session reconnect:
    snap_orders "new"/"pending_new" -> populate registry.
    orders NOT in snap_orders -> clear from registry.
  Canonical source: 0500000 dv1_80 §4 AR-053 + AR-056.

Available USD check (Gate 7 exposure check):
  available_USD = wallet_balance
                  - sum(pending_orders.values())
  If available_USD < per_trade_USD: SKIP_INSUFFICIENT_USD.

8. HARD RULES
============================================================

HR-RISK-001  Net 1:1.5 R:R is SACRED and HARDCODED as a
             MINIMUM FLOOR. CIATS does not own the floor.
             Bill does not change it. It never changes.
             Every trade must achieve at minimum 1.5
             units of net gain per 1 unit of net risk
             after fees. CIATS owns the sizing curve
             above the floor. No exception below the
             floor.

HR-RISK-002  Per-module wallet_balance for SIZING uses
             cash only. wallet_balance is the module's
             allocated spot USD cash (per TB00000 v2_49
             §9.4 operator-controlled per-module
             funding). Mark-to-market value of open
             positions is NEVER included in the sizing
             wallet.

HR-RISK-003  Per-module wallet_baseline is set ONCE at
             startup. It is NEVER updated during
             operation and NEVER reset on mid-session
             reconnect. It resets only on full TothBot
             process restart. Each module's baseline is
             independent per TB00000 v2_49 §9.7.

HR-RISK-004  Drawdown monitoring uses mark-to-market.
             current_wallet_value = cash + bid * qty
             for all open positions owned by the module.
             Bid price is used (conservative realizable
             exit value).

HR-RISK-005  Gate 7 uses available_USD not wallet_balance.
             available_USD deducts all pending order
             commitments from wallet_balance before
             checking USD sufficiency.

HR-RISK-006  Full halt does NOT auto-close positions.
             Existing resting TP and emergSL protection
             remains active through and after a halt.
             Bill decides on position disposition.

HR-RISK-007  Session pause auto-resumes.
             When per-module drawdown_pct recovers below
             session_pause_drawdown_pct, that module
             automatically resumes accepting entries.
             No manual action required.

HR-RISK-008  Full halt requires manual operator action.
             TothBot does NOT auto-resume from a full
             halt. Bill must intervene to resume
             operation. Per-module evaluation.

HR-RISK-009  BoundedSemaphore ValueError = critical bug.
             If ValueError is raised on semaphore release,
             log at CRITICAL, alert Bill immediately, and
             halt. This indicates a position accounting
             defect. It must never occur in normal
             operation.

HR-RISK-010  All financial computations use Python
             Decimal. float arithmetic is PROHIBITED for
             any price, quantity, or financial calculation
             in the Risk Engine (BP-DEC-001 through
             BP-DEC-008).

HR-RISK-011  RETIRED at dv1_0 -> dv1_1 housekeeping
             amendment (TB00317). Prior text asserted
             hardcoded Half-Kelly activation at 200
             closed trades. Per TB00000 v2_29 RETIRED
             list (carried forward through v2_49 §9.17):
             "Hardcoded Half-Kelly transition at 200
             trades - Half-Kelly remains a candidate
             sizing methodology CIATS may adopt as the
             underlying sizing-curve form. It is no
             longer a hardcoded transition rule."
             CIATS owns the sizing-curve form above the
             sacred 1:1.5 R:R floor per TB00000 v2_49
             §9.10 + §9.17.

HR-RISK-012  CIATS parameter changes require the
             200-trade floor. No risk parameter may be
             changed by CIATS below 200 closed trades.
             EWMA monitoring is active below 200 trades
             but does not drive parameter changes.
             Per-module: each module's 200-trade
             threshold is independent per TB00000 v2_49
             §9.6.

HR-RISK-013  Drawdown evaluates continuously between
             candles. Every bbo ticker event for any pair
             with an open position triggers a per-module
             drawdown evaluation. The per-wallet circuit
             breaker is not deferred to the next candle
             close.

HR-RISK-014  Pending Order Registry is reconciled at
             startup and on every mid-session reconnect.
             Stale registry entries are a capital
             accounting defect.
