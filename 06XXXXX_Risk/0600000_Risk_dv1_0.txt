============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Domain Document for AA=06 Risk. It
defines the entire Risk domain: its purpose, scope,
governing principles, hard rules, and the complete
structure of all controlled documents within it.

The Risk domain governs every rule, formula, threshold,
and decision that controls how much capital TothBot V2
commits to any trade or set of trades, when TothBot pauses
or halts due to adverse performance, and how position
concentration and capital exposure are bounded at all times.

Nothing that touches capital allocation, drawdown, or
position exposure is unspecified. Nothing is assumed.
Everything is explicitly defined in this domain or in the
instance documents beneath it.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

The Risk domain owns:

  - Position sizing formulas: tradeable_pct, per_trade_pct,
    portfolio_USD definition, Half-Kelly activation and
    formula, and the Gate 8 Position Sizer computation
  - Drawdown monitoring: portfolio_baseline_USD definition,
    drawdown formula, evaluation triggers, and the two
    circuit breaker thresholds (session pause and full halt)
  - Concentration rules: max_concurrent position limit,
    asyncio.BoundedSemaphore enforcement mechanism
  - Exposure rules: Pending Order Registry, available_USD
    computation, USD sufficiency check at Gate 7
  - Safety parameter framework: the complete registry of
    all CIATS-owned risk parameters and their starting
    values
  - The one hardcoded constraint: net 1:1.5 R:R
    This constraint is NOT CIATS-owned. It never changes.
    It is the sole parameter sacred from CIATS authority.

The Risk domain does NOT own:

  - Trading strategy signals or entry criteria (AA=07)
  - Order dispatch and execution mechanics (AA=08)
  - Exchange wire protocol (AA=04)
  - CIATS improvement cycle procedures (AA=02)
  - System component architecture (AA=05)
  - The emergSL stop-market order itself (AR-008)
    Risk defines the emergSL price. Exchange Interface
    defines the order type. WS Manager places the order.

The boundary is financial exposure. The Risk domain owns
every rule that governs how much money is at risk and under
what conditions TothBot stops committing more.

============================================================
3. GOVERNING PRINCIPLES
============================================================

3.1 Loss Prevention Takes Priority Over Profit Generation

This is the most critical mission function. Every risk
rule is designed first to prevent loss, second to enable
profit. When these objectives conflict, loss prevention
wins. This is not negotiable. It is encoded in the
1:1.5 R:R constraint, the drawdown thresholds, and the
sizing caps.

3.2 The Sacred Constraint — Net 1:1.5 R:R

Net 1:1.5 risk-to-reward is the only hardcoded constraint
in TothBot V2. CIATS does not own it. Bill does not change
it. It never changes. Every trade must target at minimum
1.5 units of net gain for every 1 unit of net risk, after
fees. This is the floor below which no trade may be placed.

  net_loss   = mae_pct * entry_fill_price
  net_gain   = net_loss * 1.5
  gross_target = net_gain + 0.16% + 0.26%
  emergSL_dist = ATR(14) * 3.0x (off-book, Layer 3 only)

All calculations use ACTUAL fill price, not limit price.
This is why OTO conditional orders were rejected (AR-008).

3.3 All Parameters Are CIATS-Owned Starting Values

Every risk parameter except net 1:1.5 R:R is a starting
value subject to data-driven optimization by CIATS. No
parameter is sacred. No parameter is permanent. CIATS
replaces every starting value with data after the 200-trade
floor is reached. PDCA governs all changes.

3.4 Cash Is the Sizing Basis — MTM Is the Drawdown Basis

Position sizing uses realized capital only (cash in hand).
Drawdown monitoring uses current portfolio value including
mark-to-market of open positions. These are two separate
computations with two different portfolio definitions.
They must never be conflated.

  Sizing:   portfolio_USD = spot_usd_balance (cash only)
  Drawdown: current_portfolio_USD = cash + sum(bid * qty)

3.5 No Parameter Change Without the 200-Trade Floor

Statistical inference is not valid below 200 closed trades.
CIATS monitoring is active below 200 trades (EWMA at 50
candle evals). CIATS parameter changes are prohibited
below 200 trades. The Proposal Engine does not activate
until the 200-trade floor is reached.

3.6 The System Does Not Auto-Close on Halt

A full halt (10% drawdown) blocks all new entries and
alerts Bill. It does NOT auto-close open positions. Bill
decides whether and how to close open positions after a
halt. Existing resting TP and emergSL protection remains
active on all open positions through and after a halt.

============================================================
4. RISK ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
============================================================

The Risk domain operates at two points in the trading
pipeline and one continuous monitoring loop:

------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Gate 7 — Risk Guard (Pipeline Entry Check)
------------------------------------------------------------

Fires on every 5-minute OHLC candle close event, after
the Selection Controller passes a candidate pair.

Gate 7 checks (in order):
  (1) Drawdown check: is drawdown_pct below both thresholds?
      If >= full_halt_drawdown (10%): HALT. Block all entries.
      If >= session_pause_drawdown (5%): PAUSE. Block entries.
      Resumes automatically when drawdown recovers below 5%.
      Full halt requires manual operator action to resume.
  (2) Concentration check: is max_concurrent not reached?
      asyncio.BoundedSemaphore enforces this limit.
      semaphore.locked() checked non-blocking at Gate 7.
  (3) Exposure check: is available_USD >= per_trade_USD?
      available_USD = spot_usd_balance
                      - sum(pending_orders.values())
      Pending Order Registry tracks all unconfirmed entries.

Gate 7 NEVER blocks on size calculation. If Gate 7 passes,
Gate 8 fires unconditionally.

------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Gate 8 — Position Sizer (Size Calculation)
------------------------------------------------------------

Fires immediately after Gate 7 pass. Never blocks.
Computes three values in Decimal arithmetic:

  order_qty:    how much base currency to buy
  tp_price:     resting TP limit price (1:1.5 R:R net)
  emergSL_price: emergency stop-market price (ATR x 3.0x)

All quantities and prices computed using Python Decimal.
All rounding uses Decimal.quantize() only. float
arithmetic and math.floor/ceil are PROHIBITED (BP-DEC-001
through BP-DEC-008 in 1011001).

------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Continuous Drawdown Monitor (Between Pipeline Events)
------------------------------------------------------------

Drawdown is NOT evaluated only at Gate 7. It is evaluated
continuously on every bbo ticker event for any pair with
an open position. This ensures a rapid market move between
pipeline evaluations triggers the halt in real time, not
at the next candle close.

Drawdown formula:
  drawdown_pct = max(0,
      (portfolio_baseline_USD - current_portfolio_USD)
      / portfolio_baseline_USD)

max(..., 0): portfolio gains are not counted as negative
drawdown. The circuit breaker is one-directional.

============================================================
5. POSITION SIZING RULES SUMMARY
============================================================

Full specification: 0611001 Position_Sizing_Rules

Starting values (all CIATS-owned except 1:1.5 R:R):

  tradeable_pct:   0.50 (50% of portfolio_USD)
  per_trade_pct:   0.05 (5% of tradeable_USD)
  max_concurrent:  20 positions

Sizing formula (Phase 1 — before 200 closed trades):
  tradeable_USD  = portfolio_USD * tradeable_pct
  per_trade_USD  = tradeable_USD * per_trade_pct
  raw_qty        = per_trade_USD / entry_limit_price
  order_qty      = raw_qty.quantize(qty_increment, ROUND_DOWN)

Sizing formula (Phase 2 — at 200+ closed trades):
  CIATS activates Half-Kelly. per_trade_pct replaced by:
    K_full = W - ((1 - W) / R)
    K_half = K_full * 0.5
    per_trade_USD = min(K_half * tradeable_USD,
                        tradeable_USD / max_concurrent)
  The cap prevents any single trade from exceeding its
  proportional share even at high Kelly fractions.

============================================================
6. DRAWDOWN AND HALT RULES SUMMARY
============================================================

Full specification: 0611002 Drawdown_and_Halt_Rules

Baseline definition:
  portfolio_baseline_USD = spot_usd_balance captured ONCE
  at startup (after snap_orders processing in Step 6 of
  startup sequence). NEVER updated during operation.
  NEVER reset on mid-session reconnect. Resets only on
  full TothBot process restart.

Current portfolio definition (for drawdown monitoring):
  current_portfolio_USD = spot_usd_balance
      + sum(bid_price[sym] * position_qty[sym]
            for sym in position_mirror)
  Uses bid price (realizable exit value). Conservative.

Circuit breakers:
  session_pause_drawdown (5%):
    Block all new entries. Alert Bill.
    Existing positions monitored normally.
    Resumes automatically when drawdown recovers below 5%.

  full_halt_drawdown (10%):
    HALT. Block all new entries. Alert Bill immediately.
    Does NOT auto-close positions. Bill decides.
    Requires manual operator action to resume.

============================================================
7. CONCENTRATION AND EXPOSURE RULES SUMMARY
============================================================

Full specification: 0611003 Concentration_and_Exposure_Rules

Max concurrent positions:
  max_concurrent: 20 (CIATS-owned starting value)
  Enforced by asyncio.BoundedSemaphore(max_concurrent).
  BoundedSemaphore raises ValueError on over-release.
  ValueError = critical bug. Log and alert immediately.
  On CIATS max_concurrent update: recreate semaphore and
  re-acquire for all currently open positions.

Pending Order Registry:
  pending_orders: dict[str, Decimal]
    maps cl_ord_id -> estimated_USD_cost
  Populated on entry dispatch:
    estimated_cost = order_qty * entry_limit_price
                     * (1 + maker_fee)
  Cleared on: exec_type=filled, expired, or canceled.
  Held (not cleared) on: exec_type=trade (partial fill
  — order still open, position partially entered).
  Reconciled at startup and on every mid-session reconnect:
    snap_orders "new"/"pending_new" -> populate registry.
    orders NOT in snap_orders -> clear from registry.

Available USD check (Gate 7 exposure check):
  available_USD = spot_usd_balance
                  - sum(pending_orders.values())
  If available_USD < per_trade_USD: SKIP_INSUFFICIENT_USD.

============================================================
8. HARD RULES
============================================================

HR-RISK-001  Net 1:1.5 R:R is SACRED and HARDCODED.
             CIATS does not own it. Bill does not change it.
             It never changes. Every trade must achieve
             at minimum 1.5 units of net gain per 1 unit
             of net risk after fees. No exception.

HR-RISK-002  portfolio_USD for SIZING uses cash only.
             spot_usd_balance (spot/main wallet, USD only).
             Mark-to-market value of open positions is
             NEVER included in the sizing portfolio.

HR-RISK-003  portfolio_baseline_USD is set ONCE at startup.
             It is NEVER updated during operation and NEVER
             reset on mid-session reconnect. It resets only
             on full TothBot process restart.

HR-RISK-004  Drawdown monitoring uses mark-to-market.
             current_portfolio_USD = cash + bid * qty
             for all open positions. Bid price is used
             (conservative realizable exit value).

HR-RISK-005  Gate 7 uses available_USD not portfolio_USD.
             available_USD deducts all pending order
             commitments from spot_usd_balance before
             checking USD sufficiency.

HR-RISK-006  Full halt does NOT auto-close positions.
             Existing resting TP and emergSL protection
             remains active through and after a halt.
             Bill decides on position disposition.

HR-RISK-007  Session pause auto-resumes.
             When drawdown_pct recovers below
             session_pause_drawdown (5%), TothBot
             automatically resumes accepting entries.
             No manual action required.

HR-RISK-008  Full halt requires manual operator action.
             TothBot does NOT auto-resume from a full halt.
             Bill must intervene to resume operation.

HR-RISK-009  BoundedSemaphore ValueError = critical bug.
             If ValueError is raised on semaphore release,
             log at CRITICAL, alert Bill immediately, and
             halt. This indicates a position accounting
             defect. It must never occur in normal operation.

HR-RISK-010  All financial computations use Python Decimal.
             float arithmetic is PROHIBITED for any price,
             quantity, or financial calculation in the Risk
             Engine (BP-DEC-001 through BP-DEC-008).

HR-RISK-011  Half-Kelly activates at 200 closed trades.
             Below 200 trades: fixed per_trade_pct applies.
             At and above 200 trades: CIATS activates
             Half-Kelly. Per-trade size updated every
             50 trades thereafter.

HR-RISK-012  CIATS parameter changes require the 200-trade
             floor. No risk parameter may be changed by
             CIATS below 200 closed trades. EWMA monitoring
             is active below 200 trades but does not drive
             parameter changes.

HR-RISK-013  Drawdown evaluates continuously between candles.
             Every bbo ticker event for any pair with an
             open position triggers a drawdown evaluation.
             The circuit breaker is not deferred to the
             next candle close.

HR-RISK-014  Pending Order Registry is reconciled at startup
             and on every mid-session reconnect. Stale
             registry entries are a capital accounting defect.

10. RELATIONSHIPS TO OTHER DOMAINS
============================================================

  AA=05 System_Architecture_Overview (0500000)
    0500000 Section 9 (Risk Rules) summarizes the Risk
    domain. The authoritative specification lives here in
    AA=06. ARs 011, 043, 051, 052, 053 in 0500000 are the
    primary source of record for the decisions captured in
    this domain. 0500000 references; AA=06 specifies.

  AA=08 Trading_System
    Gate 7 (Risk Guard) and Gate 8 (Position Sizer) are
    implemented in the Risk Engine (1011011). The Risk
    Engine consumes AA=06 specifications. AA=06 defines
    the rules. AA=08 implements them.

  AA=04 Exchange_Interface
    When a full halt is triggered, the execution mechanism
    is batch_cancel (AR-041, 0411001 WS-CAN-001 through
    -007). Risk defines the trigger condition. Exchange
    Interface defines the order type and behavior.
    Risk does not own batch_cancel. It owns the decision
    to invoke it.

  AA=02 Operations / CIATS (0211001)
    CIATS owns all risk parameters in the Parameter Store
    (except 1:1.5 R:R). CIATS writes parameter updates
    at inter-trade boundaries only. CIATS reads from Logger
    (sole data interface). Risk rules define the parameters.
    CIATS evolves their values through PDCA.

  AA=07 Strategy
    Strategy produces signals that pass through the trading
    pipeline. Risk gates those signals. Strategy does not
    define risk rules. Risk does not define signals.
    The dependency is one-way: Risk consumes Pipeline
    output, not Strategy internals.
