============================================================

This document is the Sub-Domain Anchor for the Risk Rules
sub-domain (AA=06, B=1). It anchors all TothBot V2 Risk
instance documents (0611003 through 0611004) to the Risk

hierarchy chain for the Risk Rules sub-domain, allowing

4. SUB-DOMAIN SCOPE
============================================================

The Risk Rules sub-domain (AA=06, B=1) governs the
complete specification of every risk rule that TothBot V2
enforces. This sub-domain covers concentration and
exposure limits and the safety parameter registry.
Position sizing rules are encoded canonically in 0500000
dv1_73 D5 + TB00000 v2_38 §9.10 + §9.17 per DD-012
cross-document authority hierarchy. Drawdown and halt
rules are encoded canonically in 0500000 dv1_73 AR-052 +
D7 R4 + D3 G7 CHECK 1 + AR-048 + AR-056 + TB00000 v2_38
§9.7 + §9.17 per DD-012 cross-document authority
hierarchy.

Each instance document elaborates one specific area of
the Risk rules framework:

  0611003  Concentration_and_Exposure_Rules
           The complete specification of position
           concentration limits and USD capital exposure
           tracking. Covers: per_trade_ceiling_pct
           single-trade concentration ceiling per TB00000
           v2_38 §9.10 + §9.17 (25% of module wallet
           starting, CIATS-adjustable on evidence),
           BoundedSemaphore enforcement mechanism,
           acquire/release lifecycle (Execution Engine
           acquires, position close releases), ValueError
           handling (critical bug — halt and alert), CIATS
           parameter update procedure, Pending Order
           Registry definition (cl_ord_id -> estimated_
           USD_cost), registry population on entry
           dispatch, clearing conditions (filled, expired,
           canceled), hold condition (partial fill — order
           still open), reconciliation procedure at
           startup and mid-session reconnect, and Gate 7
           available_USD sufficiency check. Primary
           source: AR-043, AR-053.

  0611004  Safety_Parameter_Framework
           The CIATS-owned risk parameter registry with
           starting values and governing rules. Per
           DD-012, the canonical parameter registry is
           TB00000 v2_38 §9.17; this instance spec
           contains the Risk-domain restatement and
           sub-domain-specific cross-references. Defines
           the sole hardcoded exception (net 1:1.5 R:R
           minimum floor — SACRED — NEVER CHANGES per
           TB00000 v2_38 §9.10). Absorbs and retires the
           old 0511005 Safety_Parameter_Review_Procedure
           content into TothBot V2 (PL-004 resolved).
