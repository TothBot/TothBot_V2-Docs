============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Sub-Domain Anchor for the Risk Rules
sub-domain (AA=06, B=1). It anchors all TothBot V2 Risk
instance documents (0611001 through 0611004) to the Risk
Domain Document (0600000).

Its purpose is structural: it completes the three-tier
hierarchy chain for the Risk Rules sub-domain, allowing
instance documents to have a valid parent chain. No
instance document in this sub-domain may be committed
to GitHub without this anchor and 0600000 already present
and approved on GitHub.

============================================================
4. SUB-DOMAIN SCOPE
============================================================

The Risk Rules sub-domain (AA=06, B=1) governs the
complete specification of every risk rule that TothBot V2
enforces. This sub-domain covers position sizing,
drawdown monitoring and circuit breakers, concentration
and exposure limits, and the safety parameter registry.

Each instance document elaborates one specific area of
the Risk rules framework:

  0611001  Position_Sizing_Rules
           The complete specification of how TothBot V2
           calculates the size of every position. Covers:
           portfolio_USD definition (cash only, spot/main
           wallet), tradeable_USD formula, per_trade_USD
           formula, Gate 8 R:R computation (mae_pct,
           net_loss, net_gain, gross_target, emergSL_dist),
           Half-Kelly formula and 200-trade activation
           threshold, concurrent position normalization
           cap, and all Decimal rounding rules for sizing
           quantities. Primary source: AR-051, AR-011.

  0611002  Drawdown_and_Halt_Rules
           The complete specification of portfolio drawdown
           monitoring and circuit breaker enforcement.
           Covers: portfolio_baseline_USD definition and
           capture timing (once at startup, after Step 6
           snap_orders), current_portfolio_USD formula
           (cash + bid * qty for all open positions),
           drawdown_pct formula (max(0, ...) — gains not
           counted), continuous evaluation trigger (every
           bbo ticker event for open-position pairs AND
           every Gate 7 evaluation), session pause (5%)
           behavior and auto-resume condition, full halt
           (10%) behavior and manual resume requirement,
           alert routing (alert@tothbot.com), and logger
           events for all threshold crossings. Primary
           source: AR-052.

  0611003  Concentration_and_Exposure_Rules
           The complete specification of position
           concentration limits and USD capital exposure
           tracking. Covers: max_concurrent position
           limit, asyncio.BoundedSemaphore enforcement
           mechanism, BoundedSemaphore acquire/release
           lifecycle (Execution Engine acquires, position
           close releases), ValueError handling (critical
           bug — halt and alert), CIATS max_concurrent
           update procedure (recreate semaphore, re-acquire
           for open positions), Pending Order Registry
           definition (cl_ord_id -> estimated_USD_cost),
           registry population on entry dispatch, clearing
           conditions (filled, expired, canceled), hold
           condition (partial fill — order still open),
           reconciliation procedure at startup and
           mid-session reconnect, and Gate 7 available_USD
           sufficiency check. Primary source: AR-043,
           AR-053.

  0611004  Safety_Parameter_Framework
           The complete CIATS-owned risk parameter registry
           with starting values and governing rules.
           Defines every risk parameter: tradeable_pct,
           per_trade_pct, max_concurrent, mae_mult,
           emergency_sl_mult, full_halt_drawdown,
           session_pause_drawdown, and the 200-trade
           Half-Kelly activation threshold. For each
           parameter: starting value, CIATS ownership
           status, activation condition, change constraint,
           and governing specification reference. Defines
           the sole hardcoded exception (net 1:1.5 R:R —
           NOT CIATS — NEVER CHANGES). Absorbs and retires
           the old 0511005 Safety_Parameter_Review_Procedure
           content into TothBot V2 (PL-004 resolved).
