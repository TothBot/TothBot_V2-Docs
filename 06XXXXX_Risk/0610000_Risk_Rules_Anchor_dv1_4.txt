This document is the Sub-Domain Anchor for the Risk Rules
sub-domain (AA=06, B=1). It anchors all TothBot V2 Risk
instance documents to the Risk Domain Document (0600000).

with only this anchor remaining as the structural chain

hierarchy chain for the Risk Rules sub-domain. The
anchor remains in force per V-039 universal parent-

the child chain is empty — the anchor binds the AA=06
sub-domain to the AA=05 Domain Document (0600000) and
is the lookup target for any future instance document
added to this sub-domain.
