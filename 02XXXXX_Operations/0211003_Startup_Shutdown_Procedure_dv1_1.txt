STARTUP SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document defines the complete, deterministic startup
and shutdown sequence for TothBot V2. Every step is
mandatory. No step may be skipped. No pipeline evaluation
may occur until the startup sequence is complete and at
least one pair reaches READY state.

This document governs WHAT TothBot V2 does on startup
and shutdown and WHY. Implementation details are in
AA=10 coding specification 1011014 Startup_Sequence_
Coding_Spec.

PAPER-AS-MASTER SCOPE.
Per TB00000 v2_38 §4.4 PA-001..010, paper trading is the
master configuration; live trading mimics paper. The
startup sequence is byte-identical paper vs live outside
the PA-004 four divergence points:
  PA-004 (1) PRIVATE WEBSOCKET CONNECTION — paper does
             NOT connect to Kraken private WS.
  PA-004 (2) ORDER DISPATCH — paper simulates locally;
             no outbound order messages.
  PA-004 (3) CAPITAL LEDGER — paper synthetic
             spot_usd_balance from paper_starting_balance.
  PA-004 (4) POSITION MIRROR SOURCING — paper populated
             exclusively by the local simulated-fill path.
The paper-mode gates in §4 are the in-startup-sequence
manifestations of these four divergence points; every
other step in §4 executes byte-identically in both modes.

3. PAIR STATES
============================================================

Every monitored pair is in exactly one of three states
at all times:

  WARM_UP:  Pair is eligible for trading but per-pair
            caches are not yet seeded. Not available for
            pipeline evaluation. Seeding in progress via
            REST GetOHLCData or incrementally via WS
            candle events. Transitions to READY when both
            per-pair caches are populated: ATR(14) and
            1H EMA(20)/EMA(50).

  READY:    Per-pair caches seeded. Pair participates in
            the full 8-gate pipeline evaluation on every
            5-minute candle close. Pipeline evaluation
            begins as soon as at least one pair reaches
            READY state.

  BLOCKED:  Pair instrument status is not online or
            post_only (i.e., reduce_only, work_in_progress,
            limit_only, delisted, or maintenance). Pair
            is excluded from all pipeline evaluation.
            Re-evaluated on each instrument channel
            update. Transitions to WARM_UP if status
            returns to online or post_only.

DAILY REGIME OWNERSHIP.
Daily regime classification is owned independently by
mod:Regime_Engine, which runs at startup and at 00:00 UTC
via daily refresh (§4 SS-START-008 NOTE + §4 SS-START-010).
Daily regime is a SYSTEM-WIDE availability condition, not
a per-pair WARM_UP-to-READY gate. Gate 3 (regime gate) in
the 8-gate pipeline blocks pipeline entry if the daily
regime classification is unavailable for the candidate
pair. (Per 0500000 dv1_75 AR-049 canonical + 1011014
dv1_8 §6 Step 8 SS-WARMUP-003 NOTE; OI-NEW-001 +
DEFECT-SS-004 closure.)

Pair-state transitions are mode-invariant. Paper trading
and live trading produce identical pair-state semantics
per PA-005.

============================================================
4. STARTUP SEQUENCE (10 STEPS)
============================================================

The startup sequence is governed by AR-049 (0500000
dv1_75). All 10 steps must complete before pipeline
evaluation begins. Steps execute in the order listed.

PAPER-MODE DISPATCH BOUNDARY.
The paper_trading_mode flag is detected at process start
per SS-PAPER-001 (1011014 dv1_8 paper-mode dispatch
section). Steps 2, 5, and 6 each contain a paper-mode
gate that skips private-WS-bound operations per PA-004
(1) + (4); the synthetic-ledger branch in Step 5 provides
the paper-mode portfolio_baseline_USD source per PA-004
(3) + SS-PAPER-003. All other steps execute byte-
identically in both modes per PA-005.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-001  STEP 1 — Kraken Status API Check
------------------------------------------------------------

Query: https://status.kraken.com/api/v2/scheduled-
       maintenances.json

Check for scheduled maintenance windows. If a maintenance
event is scheduled within 2 hours: log CRITICAL and alert
alerts@tothbot.com (OI-019 plural-alert routing per
TB00000 v2_10). This check is informational only — it
does not block startup. Bill decides whether to stand
down.

If the Status API is unavailable: log WARNING and continue.
A Status API failure does not halt startup.

Step 1 executes identically in paper and live modes per
PA-005.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-002  STEP 2 — Acquire WebSocket Token
------------------------------------------------------------

Call Kraken REST API: GetWebSocketsToken.
This token authenticates the private WebSocket connection.

The token is valid for 15 minutes. It must be refreshed
before expiry on every reconnection event. Token
acquisition failure is fatal: retry 3 times. If all
retries fail, TothBot cannot establish the authenticated
WS connection. systemd will restart TothBot on watchdog
timeout.

Reconnect-attempt-count and window for fatal-reconnect
behavior are governed by 0511001 WS_Manager_Specification
dv1_9 and 0500000 dv1_75 AR-056: 20 total attempts
spanning ~481 seconds (~8 minutes) before fatal log +
systemd restart (OI-020 propagation per TB00000 v2_10).

PAPER-MODE GATE (PA-004 (1) + SS-PAPER-002).
In paper mode, Step 2 is SKIPPED because the private
WS connection is not established (see SS-START-005
paper-mode gate). The startup sequence proceeds directly
to Step 3.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-003  STEP 3 — Connect Public WS and Subscribe
------------------------------------------------------------

Establish connection to wss://ws.kraken.com/v2.

Subscribe in a single message:
  - instrument channel (snapshot: true) — pair specs
  - status channel (snapshot: true) — engine state
  - ohlc channel, interval=5 (snapshot: true) — system
    clock
  - ohlc channel, interval=60 (snapshot: true) — 1H HTF
    cache

Do NOT subscribe ticker at this step. Ticker subscriptions
are managed per-pair after Position Mirror is restored
(Step 7).

NOTE: Kraken WS v2 rejects multiple ohlc intervals per
symbol with "Already subscribed to one ohlc interval on
this symbol." (OI-NEW-001 per 1011014 dv1_4 DEFECT-SS-004
closure.) interval=5 carries the system clock; interval=60
is the 1H HTF cache. interval=1440 daily candles are NOT
subscribed here — they are sourced via REST by
mod:Regime_Engine independently (see SS-START-008 NOTE
and SS-START-010).

Start the WS ping task (30-second interval) and zombie
detection task (90-second no-real-data threshold) on this
connection immediately after subscription.

Step 3 executes identically in paper and live modes per
PA-005. The public WS connection carries the same market
data in both modes — paper trading does not synthesize
market data.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-004  STEP 4 — Process Instrument Snapshot
------------------------------------------------------------

Wait for the instrument channel snapshot message.
Timeout: 10 seconds. On timeout: reconnect and retry
from Step 3.

For each pair in the snapshot, populate the per-pair
instrument cache: price_increment, qty_increment, qty_min,
cost_min, status. All numeric values converted to Decimal
immediately on parse.

Set initial pair state from instrument status:
  online or post_only → WARM_UP
  all other statuses  → BLOCKED

Step 4 executes identically in paper and live modes per
PA-005.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-005  STEP 5 — Connect Private WS and Subscribe
              (LIVE) / Synthetic Ledger Init (PAPER)
------------------------------------------------------------

PAPER-MODE GATE (PA-004 (1) + (3) + SS-PAPER-002 +
SS-PAPER-003).
In paper mode, Step 5 SKIPS the private WS connection
and SKIPS executions/balances channel subscription.
Instead:
  (a) Initialize the synthetic capital ledger with
      spot_usd_balance = paper_starting_balance per
      module (paper_starting_balance_long_usd = $5,000;
      paper_starting_balance_short_usd = $5,000 per
      TB00000 v2_38 §9.17).
  (b) Set portfolio_baseline_USD = paper_starting_balance
      for the relevant module wallet. This is the ONLY
      time portfolio_baseline_USD is set during a
      paper-mode TothBot process lifecycle (mirrors
      HR-SS-002 invariant).
  (c) No private WS ping task is started (no private
      connection exists). The public WS ping task
      started in Step 3 remains the sole WS keep-alive
      task in paper mode.

LIVE MODE.
Establish connection to wss://ws-auth.kraken.com/v2
using the token acquired in Step 2.

Subscribe executions channel with:
  snap_orders: true  — snapshot of all open orders
  snap_trades: true  — last 50 fills for gap reconciliation
  order_status: true — MANDATORY: without this, amended
                       and restated orders are silently
                       dropped. Non-negotiable. (AR-026)
  ratecounter: true  — real-time rate limit monitoring

Extract maxratecount from the executions subscription ACK.
Use this as the operative rate limit threshold.

Subscribe balances channel with snapshot: true.
Parse the balances snapshot: extract spot/main wallet
USD balance only. The top-level balance field is the sum
across all wallet types — it must NOT be used. Only
wallets[type="spot"][id="main"].balance is tradeable
capital (AR-050).

Set portfolio_baseline_USD = spot_usd_balance at this
exact moment. This is the ONLY time portfolio_baseline_USD
is set during a live TothBot process lifecycle. It is
never reset on reconnection. It resets only on full
process restart.

Start ping task and zombie detection on the private WS
connection.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-006  STEP 6 — Reconcile Open Positions
------------------------------------------------------------

PAPER-MODE GATE (PA-004 (4) + SS-PAPER-004).
In paper mode, Step 6 SKIPS snap_orders reconciliation.
The Position Mirror is populated EXCLUSIVELY by the
local simulated-fill path during operation; at process
start the Position Mirror is initialized empty. No
INCOMPLETE_PROTECTION check applies (no real Kraken
orders exist for paper-mode TothBot strategies). The
sequence proceeds directly to Step 7.

LIVE MODE.
Process the snap_orders snapshot received from the
executions channel subscription in Step 5.

Restore the Position Mirror from the snapshot. For each
open position found, record: symbol, entry_fill_price
(from avg_price field), quantity, TP order_id, emergSL
order_id, cl_ord_id.

If any position has a TP order but no emergSL order, or
an emergSL order but no TP order: log
INCOMPLETE_PROTECTION at CRITICAL level and alert
alerts@tothbot.com (OI-019 plural-alert routing per
TB00000 v2_10). Do not attempt to auto-repair the missing
order. Position state is ambiguous — operator
confirmation is required before any automated action.

Fallback: if the executions channel snapshot contains a
sequence gap (snap_orders data is incomplete): fall back
to REST GetOpenOrders for position reconciliation, and
REST GetAccountBalance for balance verification (AR-021).

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-007  STEP 7 — Restore Ticker Subscriptions
------------------------------------------------------------

For each pair with an open position in the Position
Mirror: subscribe ticker with event_trigger:bbo. This
activates real-time MAE monitoring using bid price (the
realizable exit price for long positions).

For all other monitored pairs: subscribe ticker with
event_trigger:trades. This provides volume data for
Gate 2 and candle updates.

Ticker subscriptions are per-pair and managed
individually. The WS Manager tracks which event_trigger
is active for each pair.

Step 7 executes identically in paper and live modes per
PA-005. In paper mode the Position Mirror is initially
empty (per SS-START-006 paper-mode gate), so all pairs
subscribe ticker with event_trigger:trades initially;
event_trigger transitions to bbo occur on simulated-fill
events thereafter.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-008  STEP 8 — REST Warm-Up (GetOHLCData)
------------------------------------------------------------

For all WARM_UP pairs, execute REST GetOHLCData calls to
seed the per-pair indicator caches. Two calls per pair,
staggered 1.1 seconds apart within the same pair to
avoid rate limit violations. Calls for different pairs
may run concurrently.

  Call A — interval=5 (5-minute candles): seed ATR(14).
    Use response[:-1] only. The last entry (response[-1])
    is the current not-yet-committed candle. Including
    it corrupts ATR and all regime calculations (AR-017).

  Call B — interval=60 (1-hour candles): seed 1H EMA(20)
    and EMA(50) for Gate 4 HTF confirmation cache. Use
    response[:-1] only.

NOTE: Call C (interval=1440 daily candles) is NOT part
of per-pair warm-up. Daily regime classification is
handled independently by mod:Regime_Engine, which runs
at startup and at 00:00 UTC via daily refresh per
SS-START-010. Per-pair warm-up contains only Call A and
Call B. (OI-NEW-001 + DEFECT-SS-004 closure per 1011014
dv1_4; canonical at 0500000 dv1_75 AR-049 + 1011014
dv1_8 §6 Step 8 SS-WARMUP-003 NOTE.)

A pair transitions from WARM_UP to READY when both of
the following are true:
  (a) ATR(14) seeded (from Call A, or after 14 candle
      close events if REST failed).
  (b) 1H EMA(20) and EMA(50) seeded (from Call B).

Daily regime availability is a system-wide condition
owned by mod:Regime_Engine and enforced at Gate 3 in
the pipeline. It is NOT a per-pair WARM_UP-to-READY gate
(per §3 DAILY REGIME OWNERSHIP).

Pairs where REST warm-up fails remain in WARM_UP and
seed incrementally via WS candle close events. This is
slower but acceptable. TothBot does not wait for all
pairs — pipeline begins as soon as Step 9 is satisfied.

Step 8 executes identically in paper and live modes per
PA-005. REST GetOHLCData carries the same market data
in both modes.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-009  STEP 9 — First READY Pair: Enter
              OPERATIONAL
------------------------------------------------------------

TothBot V2 enters OPERATIONAL state when at least one
pair reaches READY state. This is the canonical READY=1
trigger criterion, unanimous across 1011014 dv1_8
SS-STARTUP-022/026 + 0500000 dv1_75 AR-049 (9) + this
specification.

Send sd_notify READY=1 to systemd at this moment, AFTER
the first-READY-pair condition is satisfied (HR-SS-009).
READY=1 is NEVER sent before Step 9. systemd transitions
the service from activating to active upon receipt of
READY=1.

WARM_UP pairs continue seeding in the background and
transition to READY organically. BLOCKED pairs are
re-evaluated on each instrument channel update event.

Pipeline evaluation runs only for READY pairs. WARM_UP
and BLOCKED pairs are excluded at the pipeline level
before Gate 1.

Step 9 executes identically in paper and live modes per
PA-005.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-START-010  STEP 10 — Continuous Operation
------------------------------------------------------------

TothBot V2 operates continuously from OPERATIONAL state.

Daily at 00:00 UTC: mod:Regime_Engine calls GetOHLCData
(interval=1440) for all monitored pairs to refresh the
daily regime classification. Calls are staggered to
avoid rate limit violations (AR-036). The daily regime
refresh is owned by mod:Regime_Engine and is NOT a
per-pair warm-up activity — it operates independently
of the WARM_UP / READY / BLOCKED state machine.

The systemd watchdog task sends sd_notify WATCHDOG=1
every 30 seconds. systemd detects a frozen event loop
if no ping arrives within 120 seconds and restarts
TothBot automatically.

On restart: the full 10-step startup sequence executes
from Step 1.

Step 10 executes identically in paper and live modes
per PA-005.

============================================================
5. MID-SESSION RECONNECTION
============================================================

A WebSocket disconnection during live operation triggers
a reconnection sequence that is distinct from startup.
The reconnection sequence is specified in 0511001
WS_Manager_Specification dv1_9.

Reconnect-attempt-count and window for fatal-reconnect
behavior: 20 total attempts spanning ~481 seconds (~8
minutes) before FATAL_RECONNECT_FAILED is logged
CRITICAL and alerts@tothbot.com is alerted (OI-019 +
OI-020 per TB00000 v2_10; canonical at 0500000 dv1_75
AR-056 + 0511001 dv1_9 + 1011014 dv1_8 SS-RECON-015).

PAPER-MODE GATE (PA-004 (1) + SS-PAPER-006).
In paper mode, the private WS connection does not exist;
the reconnect sequence operates only on the public WS
connection. All private-WS-bound reconnect steps
(executions resubscribe, balances resubscribe,
snap_orders gap reconciliation) are SKIPPED in paper
mode. Public WS reconnect proceeds identically.

The following invariants MUST be preserved across every
reconnection event. They are stated here as operational
requirements — they are never negotiable. They apply to
both paper and live modes; mode-specific behaviors are
noted where relevant.

  INVARIANT 1: portfolio_baseline_USD is NEVER reset
    during reconnection. It was set once at startup
    (Step 5 — live-mode balances snapshot or
    paper-mode synthetic-ledger init). It survives all
    reconnection events. It resets only on full TothBot
    process restart.

  INVARIANT 2: drawdown_pct is NEVER reset during
    reconnection. Drawdown is measured continuously
    from the startup baseline. Reconnection does not
    restart the drawdown clock.

  INVARIANT 3: system_state (NORMAL, SESSION_PAUSE,
    FULL_HALT) is PRESERVED across reconnection. A
    halt condition that existed before disconnect is
    still active after reconnect.

  INVARIANT 4: Any position that closed during the
    disconnection gap (TP filled or emergSL fired
    while offline) must be detected via Position
    Mirror vs snap_orders reconciliation. The CIATS
    Trade Outcome Bus must be fired for each gap-
    closed position before the pipeline resumes. In
    paper mode this invariant degenerates trivially
    — no Kraken orders exist for paper-mode
    strategies, so no gap-closed positions can occur
    via Kraken-side fills; the simulated-fill path
    continues uninterrupted on public WS resubscribe.

  INVARIANT 5: candle close events that fired during
    the reconnection gap are DISCARDED. The system
    does not attempt to replay missed pipeline
    evaluations.

============================================================
6. SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-STOP-001  NORMAL SHUTDOWN
------------------------------------------------------------

A normal shutdown is one where TothBot is stopped
deliberately by Bill with no positions open.

Preferred sequence:
  1. Wait for all open positions to close naturally
     (TP fills, signal-based exits, or MAE exits).
  2. Confirm Position Mirror is empty.
  3. Confirm no pending orders in Pending Order
     Registry.
  4. Stop TothBot process (systemctl stop tothbot).
  5. TothBot sends graceful WS close on both
     connections (live mode) or on the public WS only
     (paper mode).
  6. systemd logs service stop.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-STOP-002  SHUTDOWN WITH OPEN POSITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

If TothBot must be stopped while positions are open
(maintenance, migration, emergency):

  MANDATORY BEFORE STOPPING (LIVE MODE):
  (a) Confirm that an active emergSL order exists on
      the Kraken matching engine for every open
      position. The emergSL provides crash protection
      during the offline period. TothBot must never be
      stopped with an unprotected open position.
  (b) Verify emergSL orders via REST GetOpenOrders or
      Kraken web interface before stopping the process.

  PAPER MODE.
  No real Kraken orders exist for paper-mode
  strategies. Stopping TothBot in paper mode does not
  expose any real capital to risk. The Position Mirror
  state is held in process memory; on restart, the
  Position Mirror initializes empty per SS-START-006
  paper-mode gate. Paper-mode open positions
  effectively vanish on shutdown (this is the expected
  behavior — paper trading does not move real money).

  PROHIBITED (LIVE MODE):
  cancel_all_orders_after (DMS) must NEVER be called
  before shutdown. Calling DMS cancels the emergSL
  orders that protect open positions — this removes
  crash protection entirely. DMS is prohibited in all
  TothBot V2 code under any circumstance (AR-001,
  AR-055).

  AFTER STOPPING (LIVE MODE):
  Open positions remain protected by the resting
  emergSL orders on the Kraken matching engine. These
  orders remain active regardless of TothBot's process
  state. Bill monitors positions manually during any
  offline period.

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-STOP-003  CRASH OR WATCHDOG RESTART
------------------------------------------------------------

On crash or watchdog-triggered restart, systemd
automatically restarts TothBot after RestartSec=5
seconds. systemd-level restart governance is bounded by
StartLimitBurst=3 attempts within
StartLimitIntervalSec=600 seconds (TB00000 v2_38 §9.17).

The full 10-step startup sequence executes on restart.

LIVE MODE.
Position Mirror is restored from snap_orders in Step 6.
Any positions that closed during the downtime gap are
detected and CIATS Trade Outcome Bus is fired for each.
During the restart window, all open positions remain
protected by their resting emergSL orders on the Kraken
matching engine. These orders fire automatically if the
stop-price is reached — no TothBot process required.

PAPER MODE.
Position Mirror initializes empty per SS-START-006
paper-mode gate. No persistence of paper-mode positions
across process restarts is provided by this
specification (consistent with paper-trading expected
behavior — no real capital at risk).

------------------------------------------------------------
SS-STOP-004  FULL HALT SHUTDOWN
------------------------------------------------------------

A Full Halt (per-wallet drawdown_pct ≥
full_halt_drawdown_pct per TB00000 v2_38 §9.7) does not
stop the TothBot process. It halts new entry pipeline
evaluations only for the affected module. Existing
positions in that module continue to be monitored and
exited by the Exit Controller. Per-wallet halts do not
cascade to other modules.

Bill decides whether to stop the TothBot process after
a Full Halt. If Bill stops TothBot after a Full Halt,
SS-STOP-002 procedure applies.

============================================================
7. HARD RULES
============================================================

  HR-SS-001  No pipeline evaluation may occur before at
             least one pair reaches READY state (Step 9).
             Applies identically in paper and live modes
             per PA-005.

  HR-SS-002  portfolio_baseline_USD is set ONCE at
             startup Step 5 (live-mode balances snapshot
             or paper-mode synthetic-ledger init per
             SS-PAPER-003). It is never reset on
             reconnection. It resets only on full
             process restart.

  HR-SS-003  snap_orders: true is the PREFERRED
             reconciliation method at live-mode startup
             and reconnect. REST GetOpenOrders is the
             fallback only (AR-003). In paper mode,
             snap_orders is not used (PA-004 (4) +
             SS-PAPER-004).

  HR-SS-004  order_status: true is MANDATORY on every
             live-mode executions channel subscription.
             It must never be omitted (AR-026). Not
             applicable in paper mode (no executions
             channel subscription per PA-004 (1) +
             SS-PAPER-002).

  HR-SS-005  response[-1] (the uncommitted current
             candle) must ALWAYS be excluded from all
             GetOHLCData calculations. Use response[:-1]
             only (AR-017). Applies identically in both
             modes.

  HR-SS-006  cancel_all_orders_after (DMS) is
             PROHIBITED in all TothBot V2 code under any
             circumstance. DMS cancels emergSL orders
             and destroys crash protection (AR-001,
             AR-055). Applies to live mode by physical
             consequence; the prohibition is universal
             at the code level per PA-005.

  HR-SS-007  TothBot must never be stopped with an open
             live-mode position that has no emergSL
             order active on the Kraken matching engine.
             Paper mode has no equivalent constraint
             (no real Kraken orders exist for paper-mode
             strategies).

  HR-SS-008  Incomplete position protection (TP without
             emergSL or vice versa) must be logged
             CRITICAL and alerted to alerts@tothbot.com.
             It must not be auto-repaired without
             operator confirmation. Applies to live mode
             only.

  HR-SS-009  sd_notify READY=1 is sent only AFTER Step 9
             (at least one READY pair). Never before any
             of Steps 1-8 complete. Applies identically
             in both modes.

  HR-SS-010  The 10-step startup sequence executes in
             full on every TothBot process start
             including crash restarts. No step is
             skippable, with the SOLE exception of the
             paper-mode gates in Step 2 (token skip per
             SS-PAPER-002), Step 5 (private-WS skip +
             synthetic-ledger init per SS-PAPER-002 +
             SS-PAPER-003), and Step 6 (snap_orders
             skip per SS-PAPER-004). These paper-mode
             gates are NOT optional skips within paper
             mode — they are mandatory mode-dependent
             behaviors.

  HR-SS-011  Paper mode does NOT connect to the Kraken
             private WS (PA-004 (1) + SS-PAPER-002).
             Step 2 (token acquisition) and Step 5
             (private WS subscribe) are skipped in
             paper mode.

  HR-SS-012  Paper mode does NOT dispatch outbound
             orders (PA-004 (2)). All order operations
             are simulated locally by the paper-mode
             simulated-fill path.

  HR-SS-013  Paper-mode portfolio_baseline_USD is the
             synthetic-ledger value paper_starting_
             balance per module (PA-004 (3) +
             SS-PAPER-003 + TB00000 v2_38 §9.17). The
             synthetic ledger uses the same fee
             schedule as live trading (FEE_MAKER_PCT,
             FEE_TAKER_PCT).

  HR-SS-014  Paper-mode Position Mirror is populated
             EXCLUSIVELY by the local simulated-fill
             path (PA-004 (4) + SS-PAPER-004). At
             process start the Position Mirror is
             initialized empty in paper mode.
