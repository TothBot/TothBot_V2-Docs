PAPER TRADING TEST SPECIFICATION
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document defines the paper trading phase requirements
for TothBot V2 (Phase 10 of the Master Plan). Paper trading
runs the full live system against live Kraken market data
with no real capital at risk. It is the final validation
gate before live trading authorization.

============================================================
3. FIVE CODING-READINESS QUESTIONS
============================================================

(1) What does paper trading DO?
    Runs TothBot V2 against live Kraken market data using
    real WS events and signals but with no real capital.
    Validates system behavior end-to-end, accumulates the
    200-trade CIATS statistical floor, and confirms all
    operational procedures work before real money is at risk.

(2) Interfaces?
    Full live Kraken WS v2 and REST connections.
    Real market data. Paper orders (or minimal test capital).
    All alert channels active (alert@tothbot.com).
    All Logger, CIATS, and Parameter Store systems active.

(3) Hard rules?
    Unit and integration tests MUST pass before paper trading.
    Minimum 200 closed trades before go-live decision.
    CIATS Proposal Engine must activate (200-trade floor).
    All exit layers must fire at least once (validated).
    Emergency SL must NEVER fire in paper trading (if it fires:
      critical defect, must investigate before live trading).
    All three drawdown circuit breakers tested.
    No critical defects outstanding at go-live.

(4) What triggers it?
    Bill decision after all unit and integration tests pass.
    Governed by 0211005 Paper_Trading_Operations_Procedure.

(5) What does it log?
    All normal Logger output. All CIATS events.
    All parameter proposals and changes.
    All alert events. Full audit trail.

============================================================
5. PAPER TRADING MINIMUM DURATION AND TRADE COUNT
============================================================

PT-DUR-001   Minimum 200 closed trades before go-live decision.
             This is the CIATS statistical inference hard floor.
             No go-live decision before 200 closed trades.

PT-DUR-002   At 200 closed trades: CIATS Proposal Engine activates.
             Half-Kelly replaces fixed per_trade_pct.
             First CIATS parameter proposals reviewed by Bill.

PT-DUR-003   At 600 closed trades (preferred — not required):
             Per-regime analysis valid. 100+ trades per bucket.
             Full regime-segmented optimization available.

PT-DUR-004   Minimum calendar duration: no floor specified.
             Trade count governs. At the system's natural trade
             frequency (5-minute candles, selective entry), 200
             trades may take weeks or months. That is acceptable.
             Do not artificially inflate trade count.

============================================================
6. VALIDATION CHECKLIST BEFORE GO-LIVE
============================================================

PT-VAL-001   Exit Controller Layer 1a: at least one of each
             trigger type observed in paper trading:
               □ HTF regime reversal exit fired
               □ Daily regime downgrade exit fired
               □ Time expiry (max_hold_candles) exit fired

PT-VAL-002   Exit Controller Layer 1b: TP fill observed.
               □ Full TP fill confirmed
               □ Partial TP fill with emergSL amendment confirmed

PT-VAL-003   Exit Controller Layer 2: MAE threshold exit observed.
               □ Layer 2 cancel sequence completed correctly

PT-VAL-004   Emergency SL: MUST NOT fire in paper trading.
             If it fires: investigate immediately. Treat as
             critical defect. Do NOT proceed to live trading
             until root cause identified and resolved.

PT-VAL-005   Drawdown circuit breakers observed:
               □ SESSION_PAUSE fired and recovered
               □ FULL_HALT fired and required operator restart

PT-VAL-006   CIATS activation confirmed:
               □ Proposal Engine active at 200+ trades
               □ At least one parameter proposal generated
               □ Half-Kelly replacing fixed per_trade_pct

PT-VAL-007   Alert email confirmed for at least one CRITICAL
             and one HIGH level event during paper trading.

PT-VAL-008   Reconnect confirmed at least once during paper
             trading period. System recovered correctly.
             No positions lost. No duplicate positions created.

PT-VAL-009   No critical defects outstanding (severity = CRITICAL
             and unresolved).

PT-VAL-010   Bill review and approval of go-live decision.

============================================================
7. GO-LIVE AUTHORIZATION

PT-GOLIVE-001  Go-live requires explicit Bill authorization.
               No automated go-live trigger. Bill decides.

PT-GOLIVE-002  Initial live trading posture:
               - 50% of portfolio tradeable (CIATS starting)
               - 5% per trade OR Half-Kelly if 200+ trades reached
               - Max 20 concurrent positions
               - Full monitoring active
               - alert@tothbot.com active

PT-GOLIVE-003  Go-live date and conditions recorded in TB session log.
