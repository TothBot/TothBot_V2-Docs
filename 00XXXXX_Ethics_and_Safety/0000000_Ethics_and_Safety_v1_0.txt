ETHICS AND SAFETY
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Ethics and Safety Domain Document for
TothBot V2. It defines the ethical constraints and safety
principles that govern the entire system — including all
code, all decisions, all documents, all parameters, and
all operator actions.

No domain, no document, no architectural decision, no
code, and no parameter value can override an ethics or
safety principle defined here. This document constrains
everything that follows it.

AA=00 Ethics_and_Safety has no dependencies. It is the
foundation upon which all other domains rest.

3. DOMAIN SCOPE
============================================================

This domain governs:

  3.1  Financial safety — rules that prevent catastrophic
       loss, protect trading capital, and ensure the system
       cannot operate beyond defined risk boundaries.

  3.2  Operational safety — rules that ensure the system
       operates reliably, fails safely, and never enters
       an undefined or unrecoverable state.

  3.3  Data integrity — rules that ensure all system
       records, logs, and audit trails are accurate,
       complete, and tamper-resistant.

  3.4  Operator ethics — rules governing Bill's conduct
       as the sole operator of a fully automated system.

  3.5  System honesty — the system must accurately report
       its own state. No condition is suppressed to avoid
       triggering an alert or breaching a threshold.

============================================================
4. ETHICS AND SAFETY PRINCIPLES
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-001  THE SACRED CONSTRAINT — NET 1:1.5 R:R
------------------------------------------------------------

The net 1:1.5 Risk:Reward ratio is the single hardcoded
constraint in TothBot V2. It is not a parameter. It is
not owned by CIATS. It cannot be changed. It cannot be
overridden by any document, any code, any parameter update,
or any operator instruction.

Every trade must target net profit = net loss × 1.5.
All calculations use actual fill price, not limit price.

This constraint exists because: trading without a minimum
positive expected value is gambling, not trading. The
1:1.5 R:R ensures that a strategy with a win rate above
40% is profitable. This floor is the ethical minimum for
running an automated trading system with real capital.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-002  CAPITAL PROTECTION IS THE PRIMARY MISSION
------------------------------------------------------------

The primary mission of TothBot V2 is to maximize profit
and minimize loss — with an explicit emphasis on minimizing
loss. Loss prevention takes priority over profit generation.
Every architectural decision, every parameter value, and
every operational procedure must be evaluated through this
lens.

A system that loses capital is a system that has failed
its primary mission regardless of how many profitable
trades it generates.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-003  NO UNDOCUMENTED BEHAVIOR
------------------------------------------------------------

TothBot V2 must not execute any action that is not
explicitly governed by a specification document. Undefined
behavior is safety-critical in an automated trading system.
If TothBot encounters a condition not covered by
specification:

  (a) The system halts that operation.
  (b) The system logs the undefined condition.
  (c) The system alerts the operator.
  (d) The system waits for operator instruction.

The system never assumes, never guesses, and never proceeds
on undefined ground.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-004  THE SYSTEM MUST SURFACE PROBLEMS
------------------------------------------------------------

Deming principle: Drive out fear. Create a system that
can report problems without penalty.

TothBot V2 must surface every problem immediately and
completely. Alerts, halt conditions, error logs, and
exception events are not failures — they are the system
working correctly. No condition may be suppressed to
avoid triggering an alert, breaching a threshold, or
notifying the operator.

A system that hides problems to appear healthy is more
dangerous than a system that fails visibly.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-005  OPERATOR ACCOUNTABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Bill is the sole operator of TothBot V2. As the sole
operator of a fully automated system trading real capital:

  (a) Bill reviews and approves all specification documents
      before they govern the system.
  (b) Bill reviews and approves all parameter changes
      before they are applied.
  (c) Bill is accountable for all system behavior.
  (d) Bill may halt the system at any time for any reason.
  (e) Bill may never override the sacred R:R constraint
      (ES-001) by any means.

No document, no code, and no CIATS recommendation can
substitute for Bill's approval of specification changes.
Claude does not self-approve documents. CIATS does not
self-approve parameter changes. Bill approves.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-006  EMERGENCY HALT AUTHORITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Bill retains unconditional authority to halt TothBot V2
at any time. The system must implement:

  (a) A manual kill switch accessible to Bill at all times:
      REST CancelAllOrders — kills all open orders instantly.
  (b) Automatic circuit breakers:
      Full halt at 10% portfolio drawdown (CIATS-owned).
      Session pause at 5% portfolio drawdown (CIATS-owned).
  (c) The kill switch and circuit breakers operate
      independently. Neither can disable the other.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-007  CRASH PROTECTION IS NON-NEGOTIABLE
------------------------------------------------------------

Open positions on Kraken must be protected against system
failure at all times. The architecture's crash protection
mechanism — resting TP limit orders and off-book emergency
SL stop-market orders on Kraken's matching engine — must
never be disabled, bypassed, or replaced.

The emergency SL must never be cancelled unless TothBot
is actively managing the exit through the Exit Controller.
Any mechanism that removes crash protection (Dead Man
Switch, FIX cancel-on-disconnect) is prohibited.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-008  DATA ACCURACY — NO FALSE RECORDS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Logger is the system's sole source of truth. All
records must be accurate. Exit reasons, fill prices, R:R
actuals, and parameter values at trade time must be
recorded truthfully. Log entries must never be deleted,
altered, or suppressed. CIATS depends on accurate records
to make correct improvement decisions. False records
corrupt the improvement engine and lead to worse outcomes.

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-009  AUTOMATION BOUNDARIES
------------------------------------------------------------

TothBot V2 is an automated system. CIATS may propose
parameter changes. CIATS may not implement parameter
changes without Bill's approval. The boundary between
autonomous operation and operator oversight is defined by:

  AUTONOMOUS (no Bill approval needed):
    - Signal generation and trade execution within
      approved specification parameters.
    - CIATS monitoring and analysis.
    - CIATS generating proposals.
    - Automatic circuit breaker activation.
    - Emergency halt triggering.
    - Alert generation and delivery.

  REQUIRES BILL APPROVAL:
    - Any parameter change proposed by CIATS.
    - Any change to specification documents.
    - Any change to risk rules.
    - Any change to the R:R constraint (prohibited).

------------------------------------------------------------
ES-010  SINGLE OPERATOR RULE
------------------------------------------------------------

TothBot V2 is designed for a single operator. Bill is
the sole operator. No other person may access the API
keys, modify the system, or instruct TothBot to take
actions. This is both an operational and ethical rule:
with automated capital management, accountability must
be singular and unambiguous.

============================================================
5. SAFETY RULES — HARD CONSTRAINTS
============================================================

These rules are absolute. No document, no code, no
operator instruction can override them.

HR-ES-001  Net 1:1.5 R:R is hardcoded. Never changes.
HR-ES-002  Emergency SL crash protection is always active
           on open positions. Never disabled.
HR-ES-003  Kill switch (CancelAllOrders) always functional.
HR-ES-004  Circuit breakers always active and functional.
HR-ES-005  Logger records are never deleted or altered.
HR-ES-006  Undefined system states trigger halt + alert.
HR-ES-007  CIATS proposals require Bill approval before
           implementation.
HR-ES-008  API keys are restricted to VPS IP. No exceptions.
HR-ES-009  No market sell is ever issued with ambiguous
           order state. Cancel confirmation required first.
HR-ES-010  TothBot never trades on paper in live mode or
           trades live in paper mode. Modes are distinct.
