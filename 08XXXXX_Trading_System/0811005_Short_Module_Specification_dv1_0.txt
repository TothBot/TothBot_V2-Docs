============================================================
SHORT MODULE SPECIFICATION
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the complete trading-module specification
for the Short Module — TothBot V2's short-side entry
dispatch and position protection placement engine. It
defines what the Short Module does, its exact order
construction rules, interfaces, hard rules, and what it
logs.

The Short Module is the short-side parallel sibling of
the Long Module (0811001). It executes the same two-phase
pattern — entry dispatch + position protection placement
— direction-symmetrically: short entries are sell-side
post_only limit orders against Kraken margin trading
(AR-009, AR-013 — Kraken Pro Intermediate tier confirmed),
short take-profit is a resting buy-side limit BELOW
entry, and short emergency stop-loss is a stop-market
buy-side trigger ABOVE entry.

The Short Module operates as a self-contained sibling
under the parallel-module framework (per TB00000 v2_29
§9.3 and 0500000 §1.3): it has its own universe scope
(margin-eligible subset of D-03), its own gate
thresholds, its own trading wallet (operator-funded
per affordable-loss), its own CIATS instance with its
own statistical pool (per TB00000 v2_29 §9.6), and its
own per-wallet drawdown halts (per TB00000 v2_29 §9.7).
Modules share the single Kraken WebSocket connection
at the data-acquisition layer only; every layer above
WS Manager is module-independent.

This document is the authoritative parent for the Short
Module portion of AA=10 coding specifications.

4. WHAT THE SHORT MODULE DOES
============================================================

The Short Module executes in two phases (direction-
symmetric to Long Module):

Phase 1 — Entry Dispatch:
  Dispatches the short-entry add_order (side="sell",
  margin trading enabled) to Kraken WS v2 after Gate 8
  authorizes the trade. Manages the Pending Order
  Registry during the GTD window. Processes all entry
  order outcomes.

Phase 2 — Position Protection Placement:
  On confirmed full entry fill: computes TP (BELOW entry)
  and emergSL (ABOVE entry) prices from actual fill
  price using the sacred 1:1.5 R:R MINIMUM floor formula
  (per TB00000 v2_29 §9.10 — minimum, not fixed; CIATS
  owns sizing curve above floor). Dispatches batch_add
  atomically. Writes completed position record to
  Position Mirror (with side="short" tag).

Input:  Gate 8 authorized short-side dispatch (symbol,
        entry_qty, entry_limit_price, ATR(14), mae_mult,
        emergency_sl_mult, leverage_cap_short,
        price_increment, qty_min, cost_min,
        margin_eligible_flag).
Output: On fill — open short position in Position Mirror
        (side="short" tagged).
        On no-fill — no position. Logging only.

Direction-symmetric mapping vs Long Module (0811001):

  Direction             Long (0811001)    Short (this doc)
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  Entry side            "buy"             "sell"
  Entry limit rounding  ROUND_DOWN        ROUND_UP
                                          (AR-069 direction-
                                          symmetric)
  TP placement          ABOVE entry       BELOW entry
                                          (AR-007)
  TP order side         "sell"            "buy"
  Emergency SL          BELOW entry       ABOVE entry
                                          (AR-008)
  Emergency SL side     "sell"            "buy"
  MAE formula           entry - bid       ask - entry
                                          (AR-048)
  Universe scope        Full D-03         Margin-eligible
                        (USD/USDC/USDT)   subset of D-03
  Leverage              N/A (spot)        3x starting
                                          (CIATS-owned,
                                          leverage_cap_short)
  RSI thresholds        30 / 50           70 / 50 (mirror)
  R:R floor             1:1.5 minimum     1:1.5 minimum
                                          (sacred, identical)
  Position Mirror tag   side="long"       side="short"
  Wallet                long_wallet       short_wallet
                                          (operator-funded)
  CIATS instance        Long CIATS        Short CIATS

============================================================
5. PHASE 1 — ENTRY DISPATCH
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 cl_ord_id Assignment (AR-002 — direction-symmetric)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-001  cl_ord_id MUST be assigned before add_order is
        dispatched. Not after. Not on ACK. Before dispatch.
        Identical rule to Long Module LM-001; the
        cl_ord_id namespace is shared across modules.
        Short Module's cl_ord_id values are written to
        Position Mirror with side="short" tag at dispatch.

SM-002  cl_ord_id format: symbol prefix (stripped of /
        and USD/USDC/USDT suffix) + millisecond timestamp +
        "S" suffix to disambiguate short-side from
        long-side ids in audit logs.
        Maximum 18 characters (Kraken limit).
        Example: "BTCUSD1712230000S"
        Short-disambiguation suffix is local convention;
        Kraken-side the value is opaque.

SM-003  cl_ord_id is recorded in Position Mirror and
        Pending Order Registry at dispatch time. Before
        Kraken ACK. No async gap (A-2, record-at-dispatch).
        Position Mirror record carries side="short" tag.

SM-004  tp_cl_ord_id and emgsl_cl_ord_id for batch_add
        are assigned at the same time as entry cl_ord_id
        (before dispatch). All three recorded in Position
        Mirror at dispatch with side="short" tag.
        Kraken-assigned order_ids are populated later on
        batch_add ACK.

------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 add_order Construction (AR-006 — direction-symmetric)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-005  Short entry add_order MUST conform to this exact
        format. No field may be omitted. No extra fields.

        {
          "method": "add_order",
          "params": {
            "order_type":    "limit",
            "side":          "sell",
            "symbol":        <symbol>,
            "limit_price":   <entry_limit_price_str>,
            "order_qty":     <entry_qty_quantized_str>,
            "post_only":     true,
            "time_in_force": "gtd",
            "expire_time":   <ISO 8601 UTC now + entry_timeout_sec>,
            "cl_ord_id":     <assigned cl_ord_id>,
            "stp_type":      "cancel_newest",
            "deadline":      <ISO 8601 UTC now + 5s>,
            "margin":        true
          },
          "req_id": <assigned req_id>
        }

        Direction-symmetric differences from Long Module
        LM-005:
          - side="sell" (LM-005 has "buy")
          - margin:true (LM-005 omits the margin field —
            Long is unleveraged spot)
        All other fields identical to LM-005.

SM-006  stp_type MUST be "cancel_newest" with underscore.
        WS v2 ALWAYS uses underscore format. REST API uses
        hyphen (cancel-newest). Never confuse the two.
        Identical rule to LM-006.

SM-007  deadline MUST be ISO 8601 UTC string = now + 5s.
        Engine rejects message arriving after this time.
        Do NOT omit deadline. Do NOT use a longer window.
        Identical rule to LM-007.

SM-008  post_only MUST be true. Guarantees maker fee
        (0.16%). If the sell order would cross the spread
        on arrival (limit price <= current bid), Kraken
        rejects with exec_type=canceled reason=post_only.
        This is correct behavior — log and record
        ENTRY_POST_ONLY_REJECTED. Identical rule to LM-008
        with direction-appropriate cross-the-spread
        condition (long: limit_price >= ask; short:
        limit_price <= bid).

SM-009  time_in_force MUST be "gtd". expire_time =
        ISO 8601 UTC now + entry_timeout_sec. CIATS-owned
        starting value: 30-60 seconds per TB00000 v2_29
        §9.17 (Short_Module gets its own entry_timeout_sec
        instance per per-module instantiation rule).
        Unfilled order expires automatically. No manual
        cancel required.

SM-010  All price and quantity values MUST be sent as
        strings with the precision of price_increment
        (for prices) and qty_min (for quantities).
        Decimal(str()) throughout. No float values.
        Identical rule to LM-010.

SM-011  Entry limit price formula (AR-069 direction-
        symmetric):
          entry_limit_price_raw = last_complete_candle
                                   [symbol].close
          entry_limit_price = entry_limit_price_raw
                              .quantize(price_increment,
                                        rounding=ROUND_UP)
        Conservative rounding for short post_only fill:
        higher limit → more likely SELL post_only fill
        above bid. Direction-symmetric to LM (long uses
        ROUND_DOWN to keep BUY limit below ask).

------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Pending Order Registry (AR-053 — direction-symmetric)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-012  On short add_order dispatch:
          margin_required = (entry_qty * entry_limit_price
                             * (1 + maker_fee_rate))
                            / leverage_cap_short
          pending_orders[entry_cl_ord_id] = margin_required
        Margin trading allocates margin_required (not full
        notional) from the Short wallet. Leverage divisor
        applies: leverage_cap_short = 3x starting per
        TB00000 v2_29 §9.17 (CIATS-owned). Gate 7
        available_short_margin uses this registry to
        prevent margin overcommitment on simultaneous
        short entries.

SM-013  Pending Order Registry is maintained by WS Manager.
        Short Module instructs WS Manager to populate it
        at dispatch with side="short" tag. WS Manager
        clears it on fill or expiry. Long and Short
        modules share the WS Manager singleton but
        maintain separate registry entries keyed by
        cl_ord_id; side tag prevents cross-module
        contamination.

------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 Entry Order Outcomes
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-014  exec_type = filled (full fill):
          Trigger Phase 2. Extract qty = Decimal(str(cum_qty))
          and entry_fill_price = Decimal(str(avg_price)).
          Clear pending_orders[entry_cl_ord_id].
          Log ENTRY_FILLED_SHORT.

SM-015  exec_type = trade (partial fill, order still open):
          Order is still active. Hold Pending Order Registry.
          Do NOT dispatch batch_add yet.
          Log ENTRY_PARTIAL_TRADE_SHORT (cum_qty, avg_price).

SM-016  exec_type = expired OR canceled, cum_qty == 0:
          No fill. Clear Pending Order Registry.
          Log ENTRY_EXPIRED_NO_FILL_SHORT or
          ENTRY_CANCELED_NO_FILL_SHORT.
          No position opened.

SM-017  exec_type = expired OR canceled, cum_qty > 0:
          Unprotected short partial fill. Trigger partial
          fill protection immediately per AR-054 direction-
          symmetric. See Section 5.5.
          Log ENTRY_EXPIRED_PARTIAL_FILL_SHORT or
          ENTRY_CANCELED_PARTIAL_FILL_SHORT.

SM-018  exec_type = canceled, reason = post_only:
          Sell limit crossed spread on arrival (was <=
          current bid). Clear registry.
          Log ENTRY_POST_ONLY_REJECTED_SHORT.
          Short CIATS EWMA Monitor:
          short_rejection_rate_by_regime increments
          (per-module CIATS instance per TB00000 v2_29
          §9.6 — independent of long_rejection_rate_by_
          regime).

SM-019  exec_type = new (order accepted on book):
          No action required. Log ENTRY_ACCEPTED_ON_BOOK_SHORT.

------------------------------------------------------------
5.5 Partial Fill Protection (AR-054 — direction-symmetric)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-020  Triggered when exec_type=expired or canceled AND
        cum_qty > 0. An unprotected short position exists.
        Execute immediately:
          (1) Extract qty = Decimal(str(cum_qty)) and
              entry_fill_price = Decimal(str(avg_price)).
          (2) Validate qty >= qty_min AND
              qty * entry_fill_price >= cost_min.
              If fails: dispatch market BUY immediately
              (close-short cover). Log PARTIAL_FILL_BELOW_
              MINIMUM_SHORT. Fire Short CIATS Trade
              Outcome Bus.
          (3) Compute short TP (BELOW entry) and emergSL
              (ABOVE entry) using actual entry_fill_price
              (see Section 6.2).
          (4) Dispatch batch_add (TP + emergSL).
          (5) Write Position Mirror with side="short".
          Log ENTRY_PARTIAL_FILL_PROTECTED_SHORT.

============================================================
6. PHASE 2 — POSITION PROTECTION PLACEMENT
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Trigger Condition
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-021  Phase 2 triggers on exec_type=filled ONLY.
        Not on exec_type=trade (partial — wait for filled).
        Not on any other exec_type.
        Identical rule to LM-020.

------------------------------------------------------------
6.2 R:R Computation (AR-008, AR-011 — direction-symmetric)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-022  ALL R:R calculations MUST use actual fill price
        (avg_price from exec_type=filled event).
        NEVER the limit price. OTO was rejected because
        it uses limit price — R:R violation (AR-008).
        Identical rule to LM-021.

SM-023  Short-direction R:R formula (sacred 1:1.5 MINIMUM
        floor — CIATS-exempt at the floor; CIATS owns
        sizing curve above floor per TB00000 v2_29 §9.10):

          entry_fill_price = Decimal(str(avg_price))
          mae_dist         = entry_atr_14 * mae_mult
          emergSL_dist     = entry_atr_14 * emergency_sl_mult

          (Direction-symmetric: long uses bid for MAE,
           short uses ask. Fee schedule identical.)

          net_loss    = mae_dist / entry_fill_price
                        + Decimal("0.0016")   (entry maker)
                        + Decimal("0.0026")   (exit taker)

          net_gain    = net_loss * Decimal("1.5")
                        *** SACRED MINIMUM FLOOR ***
                        *** CIATS may allocate larger size
                            on expected R:R above floor;
                            CIATS may NEVER propose, size,
                            or accept a trade with expected
                            R:R below 1:1.5 (rule A1 per
                            TB00000 v2_29 §9.10) ***

          gross_tp    = entry_fill_price * (Decimal("1")
                        - net_gain
                        - Decimal("0.0016"))  (TP maker exit)

          tp_price    = gross_tp.quantize(price_increment,
                        rounding=ROUND_DOWN)
                        (rounding DOWN puts short TP
                         further from entry — conservative
                         net_gain preservation;
                         direction-symmetric to long which
                         rounds UP)

          emergSL_trigger = (entry_fill_price + emergSL_dist
                        ).quantize(price_increment,
                        rounding=ROUND_UP)
                        (rounding UP puts short emergSL
                         further from entry — conservative
                         crash-protection distance;
                         direction-symmetric to long
                         which rounds DOWN)

        Note: emergSL_dist (ATR x 3.0x starting) is NEVER
        in the R:R formula. It is crash protection only.

        Note on directionality of TP and SL:
          Long position profits when price RISES — TP is
          ABOVE entry, emergSL is BELOW entry.
          Short position profits when price FALLS — TP is
          BELOW entry, emergSL is ABOVE entry.
          The 1:1.5 R:R ratio is the same in absolute
          value; the directionality of placement is
          mirrored.

SM-024  Decimal arithmetic throughout. No float. All
        string-converted inputs before any computation.
        No accumulation of floating-point rounding error.
        Identical rule to LM-023.

------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 batch_add Construction (AR-007, AR-046, AR-032, AR-033
    — direction-symmetric)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-025  TP + emergSL dispatched atomically via batch_add
        on confirmed full fill. No sequential placement.
        No gap between TP and emergSL. One message, two legs.
        Identical timing rule to LM-024.

SM-026  Short batch_add format:
        {
          "method": "batch_add",
          "params": {
            "orders": [
              {
                "order_type": "limit",
                "side":       "buy",
                "symbol":     <symbol>,
                "limit_price": <tp_price_str>,
                "order_qty":   <entry_fill_qty_str>,
                "post_only":   true,
                "cl_ord_id":   <tp_cl_ord_id>,
                "stp_type":    "cancel_newest",
                "deadline":    <ISO 8601 UTC now + 5s>,
                "margin":      true,
                "reduce_only": true
              },
              {
                "order_type": "stop-loss",
                "side":       "buy",
                "symbol":     <symbol>,
                "order_qty":  <entry_fill_qty_str>,
                "cl_ord_id":  <emgsl_cl_ord_id>,
                "stp_type":   "cancel_newest",
                "deadline":   <ISO 8601 UTC now + 5s>,
                "margin":     true,
                "reduce_only": true,
                "triggers": {
                  "reference":  "last",
                  "price":      <emergSL_trigger_str>,
                  "price_type": "static"
                }
              }
            ],
            "token":  <ws_auth_token>,
            "req_id": <assigned req_id>
          }
        }

        Direction-symmetric differences from Long Module
        LM-025:
          - TP leg side="buy" (LM-025 has "sell") — buying
            back closes short
          - emergSL leg side="buy" (LM-025 has "sell") —
            buying back stops short loss
          - margin:true on both legs (LM-025 omits)
          - reduce_only:true on both legs (closes existing
            short; never opens new long)
        All other fields direction-symmetric or identical.

SM-027  triggers.reference MUST be "last" on emergSL leg.
        "last" = last traded price. Always available on
        Kraken matching engine, including crash scenarios.
        "index" uses external price feeds which may be
        unavailable during crash. NEVER use "index" or
        "mark" for emergSL (AR-046). Identical rule to
        LM-026.

SM-028  batch_add rate cost = +(n/2) rounded up.
        Two orders = +1 rate counter unit.
        Identical rule to LM-027.

------------------------------------------------------------
6.4 Position Mirror Write (AR-002, AR-052 — direction-
    symmetric, side-tagged)
------------------------------------------------------------

SM-029  Position Mirror record structure written on
        short entry dispatch (cl_ord_id fields) and
        updated on fill (entry_fill_price, qty, order_ids):
          symbol, side="short", entry_fill_price, qty,
          leverage_used (= leverage_cap_short at dispatch),
          tp_order_id, tp_cl_ord_id,
          emgsl_order_id, emgsl_cl_ord_id,
          entry_timestamp_utc, entry_atr_14,
          asset_regime, market_regime,
          signal_params, hold_candle_count (0 at entry),
          module="Short_Module"

        side="short" tag is the discriminator that
        Exit Controller and Position Mirror readers use
        to apply the correct direction-symmetric formulas
        (MAE = ask - entry; close = buy; TP below; SL
        above). Long and Short positions coexist in the
        same Position Mirror keyed by symbol+side.

SM-030  tp_order_id and emgsl_order_id populated from
        batch_add ACK response. Not from add_order ACK.
        cl_ord_id values populated at dispatch (before ACK).
        Identical rule to LM-029.

7. INTERFACES
============================================================

7.1 From Risk Engine (0611001 / 0611003)
  Receives: Gate 8 authorization with entry_qty, mae_mult,
            emergency_sl_mult, leverage_cap_short,
            entry_limit_price, margin_eligible_flag.
            Risk Engine evaluates Short-side Gate 8 using
            Short_Module's own per-trade sizing function
            (CIATS-owned, R:R-driven per TB00000 v2_29
            §9.10) and Short wallet balance.
  Receives: Gate 7 SHORT_WALLET_FULL_HALT signal (blocks
            new short dispatch; per-wallet halt per
            TB00000 v2_29 §9.7 — independent of Long
            wallet halt).

7.2 From WS Manager (0511001)
  Receives: executions channel events routed by exec_type
            and side tag. WS Manager dispatches short-side
            executions events to Short Module based on
            the cl_ord_id namespace + Position Mirror side
            tag lookup.
  Receives: entry_atr_14, price_increment, qty_min,
            cost_min from pre-computation cache (read at
            pipeline start, I-2).
  Receives: margin_eligible_flag from pair_cache (AR-073)
            populated at startup via REST GetAssetPairs
            margin field. Short Module dispatch is GATED
            on margin_eligible_flag == true; non-eligible
            pairs are skipped at Gate 8 evaluation in
            Risk Engine.

7.3 To WS Manager (0511001)
  Sends: short add_order instructions with margin:true.
  Sends: short batch_add instructions on fill with
         reduce_only:true on both legs.
  Sends: Pending Order Registry populate/clear instructions
         keyed by cl_ord_id + side="short" tag.

7.4 To Position Mirror (0511005)
  Writes: short PositionRecord at dispatch and on fill
          (side="short" tagged).

7.5 To Exit Controller (0811003)
  Position Mirror is the shared state. Short Module writes.
  Exit Controller reads short positions via side="short"
  filter and applies direction-symmetric exit formulas
  (Layer 1 signal-based, Layer 1 TP, Layer 2 MAE with
  short formula = ask - entry_fill_price, Layer 3
  emergency SL). No direct inter-module call.

7.6 To Logger (0511006)
  All log events including Short CIATS Trade Outcome Bus
  data. Short CIATS instance per TB00000 v2_29 §9.6 —
  Logger routes short trade outcomes to Short CIATS pool
  independent of Long pool. Cross-module data contamination
  is prohibited.

============================================================
8. HARD RULES
============================================================

HR-SM-001  cl_ord_id MUST be assigned and written to
           Position Mirror (with side="short" tag) before
           short add_order dispatch. Never after. Never
           on ACK. Before dispatch only. Direction-
           symmetric to HR-LM-001.

HR-SM-002  stp_type MUST be "cancel_newest" (underscore).
           WS v2 ALWAYS uses underscore. REST uses hyphen.
           Any code review must flag "cancel-newest" in
           WS v2 context as a defect. Identical to HR-LM-002.

HR-SM-003  batch_add MUST fire on exec_type=filled ONLY.
           Never on trade (partial). Never on expired.
           Exception: partial fill protection AR-054 uses
           batch_add after expiry + cum_qty > 0. Identical
           to HR-LM-003.

HR-SM-004  ALL R:R calculations MUST use actual fill price
           (avg_price). Never limit_price. This is sacred
           and cannot be changed by CIATS or Bill.
           Identical to HR-LM-004.

HR-SM-005  net_gain = net_loss × 1.5 is the SACRED MINIMUM
           floor (per TB00000 v2_29 §9.10). The 1.5
           multiplier as a MINIMUM is hardcoded and never
           parameterized. CIATS may compute a larger
           multiplier on a per-trade basis when expected
           R:R exceeds 1:1.5; CIATS may NEVER apply a
           multiplier below 1.5. The minimum floor is the
           only hardcoded constraint; the sizing curve
           ABOVE the floor is CIATS-owned. Direction-
           symmetric to HR-LM-005.

HR-SM-006  emergSL triggers.reference MUST be "last".
           Never "index". Never "mark". Never API default.
           Identical to HR-LM-006.

HR-SM-007  All Decimal arithmetic throughout. No float
           values anywhere in price or quantity computation.
           Decimal(str()) on all WS message numeric fields.
           Identical to HR-LM-007.

HR-SM-008  Dead Man's Switch is PROHIBITED. TothBot V2
           MUST NOT call cancel_all_orders_after at any
           time. DMS cancels emergSL — removes the crash
           protection it is meant to provide (AR-055).
           Identical to HR-LM-008.

HR-SM-009  margin:true MUST be set on every Short Module
           add_order and on both legs of the batch_add.
           Omission produces a spot-side order which
           Kraken accepts only if the seller already holds
           the asset — defeats short-trading semantics.
           Verify at code-review time.

HR-SM-010  reduce_only:true MUST be set on both legs of
           the Short Module batch_add (TP buy and emergSL
           buy). Both legs are position-closing buys
           against an open short. reduce_only:true
           guarantees the buy cannot accidentally open
           a long position if the short is already closed
           by the other leg. Long Module has no analogous
           rule because long-side TP and emergSL are
           sells that cannot accidentally open new
           positions in the same direction.

HR-SM-011  Short_Module is GATED on margin_eligible_flag
           from pair_cache. Non-margin-eligible pairs
           (subset of D-03 USD/USDC/USDT universe that
           Kraken does NOT support for margin) MUST be
           skipped at Gate 8 evaluation. Attempting a
           short order on a non-margin-eligible pair
           returns a Kraken API error and contaminates
           pending_orders. Risk Engine Gate 8 enforces
           this gate before dispatch.

HR-SM-012  Short_Module operates against its own wallet
           (short_wallet, operator-funded). Cross-wallet
           transfers between Long and Short are PROHIBITED
           by TothBot logic; Bill is the only party who
           can fund or rebalance wallets manually outside
           TothBot. Per TB00000 v2_29 §9.4 wallet funding
           is the primary loss control.

HR-SM-013  Short_Module CIATS instance owns its own
           statistical pool. Short trade outcomes feed
           ONLY Short CIATS; never Long CIATS. Cross-
           module data contamination is prohibited per
           TB00000 v2_29 §9.6. The 200-trade inference
           threshold and 600-trade per-regime threshold
           accumulate independently for Short_Module.

HR-SM-014  Short_Module drawdown halts evaluate against
           short_wallet only. session_pause_drawdown_pct
           (5% starting per TB00000 v2_29 §9.17,
           CIATS-owned) and full_halt_drawdown_pct (10%
           starting, CIATS-owned) are applied to
           short_wallet balance independently of long_wallet.
           A Short_Module full halt does NOT halt
           Long_Module and vice versa. Per TB00000 v2_29
           §9.7.

============================================================
9. CIATS PARAMETERS
============================================================

Per TB00000 v2_29 §9.17 parameter classification and
§9.6 per-module CIATS framework, all parameters below
are CIATS-OWNED starting values instantiated for
Short_Module's own CIATS instance. Values evolve
independently of Long_Module's instances.

  entry_timeout_sec:         30-60s GTD starting
                             (CIATS-owned)
  mae_mult:                  1.5x ATR(14) starting
                             (CIATS-owned, Risk domain)
  emergency_sl_mult:         3.0x ATR(14) starting
                             (CIATS-owned, Risk domain)

11. FIVE CODING-READINESS QUESTIONS
============================================================

(1) What does the Short Module DO?
    Dispatches short entry add_order (side="sell",
    margin:true) after Gate 8 authorization. On full
    fill: computes TP (BELOW entry) and emergSL (ABOVE
    entry) prices from actual fill price using sacred
    1:1.5 R:R MINIMUM floor (CIATS owns sizing curve
    above floor). Dispatches batch_add atomically with
    reduce_only:true on both legs. Writes Position
    Mirror with side="short" tag. Handles all entry
    order outcomes including partial fill protection
    (emergency market BUY cover on below-minimum
    partials).

(2) What are its interfaces?
    FROM: Risk Engine (Gate 8 params including
    leverage_cap_short and margin_eligible_flag),
    WS Manager (executions events filtered by side
    tag, ATR/price cache reads, margin field from
    pair_cache).
    TO: WS Manager (short order instructions),
    Position Mirror (write with side="short"),
    Logger (all short events to Short CIATS pool).

(3) What are its hard rules?
    HR-SM-001 cl_ord_id at dispatch with side tag.
    HR-SM-002 stp_type underscore.
    HR-SM-003 batch_add on filled only (except AR-054).
    HR-SM-004 Actual fill price for R:R.
    HR-SM-005 net_gain = net_loss × 1.5 SACRED MINIMUM
      floor (CIATS may go above; never below).
    HR-SM-006 reference="last" on emergSL.
    HR-SM-007 Decimal throughout.
    HR-SM-008 No DMS.
    HR-SM-009 margin:true on all orders.
    HR-SM-010 reduce_only:true on TP and emergSL.
    HR-SM-011 margin_eligible_flag gate at Gate 8.
    HR-SM-012 Wallet isolation (short_wallet only).
    HR-SM-013 CIATS instance isolation (Short pool only).
    HR-SM-014 Drawdown halt evaluated against
      short_wallet only.

(4) What triggers it and what does it trigger?
    Triggered by Gate 8 authorized short-side dispatch
    signal (every pipeline evaluation that passes all
    8 gates AND has direction=short — including margin-
    eligibility check at Gate 8). Triggers: WS Manager
    send short add_order, then on fill: WS Manager send
    short batch_add (TP buy + emergSL buy reduce_only),
    Position Mirror write (side="short"), Logger events
    (routed to Short CIATS pool).

(5) What does it log?
    All short entry outcomes (filled, partial, expired,
    canceled, post_only). Short partial fill protection
    events (emergency cover-buy). Short TP and short
    emergSL placement confirmations (buy-side). Short
    position opened record with leverage_used and
    side="short" tag.
