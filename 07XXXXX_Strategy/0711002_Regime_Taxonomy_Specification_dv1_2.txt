3. REGIME TAXONOMY OVERVIEW
============================================================

TothBot V2 classifies every monitored pair into one of six
regimes at 00:00 UTC daily. Regime classification is the
primary market context filter — it determines which pairs
TothBot will consider for entry and what position size
modification applies.

The 6-regime grid is a 3x2 matrix:

  Rows:    3 directional states (trend direction + strength)
  Columns: 2 volatility states (ATR percentile rank)

  Every pair occupies exactly one regime at any time.
  Regime is updated once per day at 00:00 UTC.
  Regime does NOT change intra-day.

IMPORTANT: Regime is computed independently for two
purposes:
  (1) asset_regime: the pair being evaluated for entry
  (2) market_regime: BTC/USD as market-wide context proxy

Every trade is tagged with BOTH at entry time.

============================================================
4. THE 6-REGIME GRID
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-001  DIRECTIONAL STATE DEFINITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Directional state is determined by ADX(14) and the
relationship between EMA(20) and EMA(50) on DAILY candles.

  TRENDING_POSITIVE:
    ADX(14) > adx_threshold (25)
    AND daily EMA(20) > daily EMA(50)
    Interpretation: strong trend, upward direction.

  TRENDING_NEGATIVE:
    ADX(14) > adx_threshold (25)
    AND daily EMA(20) < daily EMA(50)
    Interpretation: strong trend, downward direction.

  NON_DIRECTIONAL:
    ADX(14) <= adx_threshold (25)
    Interpretation: no strong trend. EMA relationship
    is not evaluated when ADX is below threshold.

  Parameters (CIATS-owned starting values):
    adx_threshold:   25
    htf_ema_short:   20   (daily EMA short period)
    htf_ema_long:    50   (daily EMA long period)

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-002  VOLATILITY STATE DEFINITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Volatility state is determined by the percentile rank
of ATR(14) within a 50-day rolling window of ATR values.

  NORMAL_VOL:
    ATR(14) daily percentile <= atr_percentile_thresh (67th)
    Interpretation: current volatility is at or below
    the 67th percentile of the past 50 days.

  ELEVATED_VOL:
    ATR(14) daily percentile > atr_percentile_thresh (67th)
    Interpretation: current volatility is above the 67th
    percentile of the past 50 days. Risk of whipsaw
    and stop-out is elevated.

  Parameters (CIATS-owned starting values):
    atr_percentile_thresh:  67th percentile
    atr_rolling_window:     50 days

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-003  6-REGIME GRID — COMPLETE TAXONOMY
------------------------------------------------------------

               NORMAL_VOL          ELEVATED_VOL
               (ATR <= 67th)       (ATR > 67th)
              +-------------------+-------------------+
TRENDING_POS  | REGIME 1          | REGIME 2          |
(ADX>25,      | FULL position     | FULL position     |
EMA20>EMA50)  | LONG PERMITTED    | LONG PERMITTED    |
              | (Gate 8 standard) | (ATR adapts size) |
              +-------------------+-------------------+
NON_DIRECTIONAL| REGIME 3         | REGIME 4          |
(ADX<=25)     | 50% position      | NO ENTRY          |
              | LONG PERMITTED    | Gate 3 BLOCKS     |
              | (Gate 6 sizes)    | (both directions) |
              +-------------------+-------------------+
TRENDING_NEG  | REGIME 5          | REGIME 6          |
(ADX>25,      | LONG blocked      | LONG blocked      |
EMA20<EMA50)  | SHORT permitted   | SHORT permitted   |
              +-------------------+-------------------+

Per-direction entry permission (directional cascade per
HR-REGIME-007 amended dv1_1):

  LONG entry-permitted regimes:   Regime 1, Regime 2, Regime 3.
  SHORT entry-permitted regimes:  Regime 5, Regime 6.
  Entry-blocked regimes (both):   Regime 4.

Gate 3 (Regime Pre-Filter) per directional cascade:
  Regime 4 blocked (both directions — high vol whipsaw
    territory; no directional thesis).
  Regimes 5, 6 dispatch to Short_Module (LONG blocked;
    SHORT permitted via Short_Module per AR-009 amended
    dv1_41 short via Kraken margin trading; AR-072
    short SC-GATE-1 variant; AR-073 per-side state
    management).
  Regimes 1, 2, 3 dispatch to Long_Module.
Gate 6 (Regime Gate) handles 50% sizing for Regime 3 (Long).
Gates 3 and 6 are specified in AA=08 (Trading System).
This document defines the regime state. AA=08 evaluates it.

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-004  ENTRY BLOCKING RULES (DIRECTIONAL CASCADE)
------------------------------------------------------------

  TRENDING_NEGATIVE (all vol):   DIRECTIONAL CASCADE at Gate 3.
    LONG entry: BLOCKED. Entering long in a confirmed
      downtrend is directionally incorrect.
    SHORT entry: PERMITTED via Short_Module dispatch.
      AR-009 amended dv1_41 — short via Kraken margin
      trading per DEC-011 Stage M ratification.
      AR-072 short SC-GATE-1 variant — Selection
      Controller short-side gate set.
      AR-073 per-side state management + pair_cache
      margin_eligible_flag.

  NON_DIR + ELEVATED_VOL:        BLOCKED at Gate 3.
    Both directions blocked. Reason: high volatility
    with no directional trend is whipsaw territory.
    Risk-adjusted expectancy is negative for this
    combination on either side.

  NON_DIR + NORMAL_VOL:          LONG PERMITTED at Gate 3.
    Modified: 50% of normal position size (Gate 6).
    Reason: no confirmed trend but acceptable volatility.
    Reduced size reflects lower conviction.
    Short side: not permitted — short thesis requires
    confirmed directional state (TRENDING_NEGATIVE).

  TRENDING_POS + NORMAL_VOL:     LONG PERMITTED at Gate 3.
    Standard: 100% position size.
    Reason: confirmed trend, acceptable volatility.
    Optimal regime for TothBot V2 long-side strategy.

  TRENDING_POS + ELEVATED_VOL:   LONG PERMITTED at Gate 3.
    Standard: 100% position size (ATR already adapts
    via mae_mult in Gate 8 Position Sizer — AR-043).
    Reason: confirmed trend. ATR adapts the risk distance
    automatically. Entry permitted.

5. DATA SOURCE AND TIMING
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-005  Data Source
------------------------------------------------------------

Daily regime is computed from DAILY candles retrieved via
REST API. (FLAG 2 — FP/DP verified, dv1_5.)

  API call: GetOHLCData
  Endpoint: per 0411002 Kraken_REST_API_Specification
  Interval: 1440 (daily candles, minutes per period)
  Pairs:    one call per monitored pair
  Returns:  720 committed daily candles in response[:-1]

Daily candles are NOT available from the WS ohlc channel
(ohlc channel provides 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h intervals
only). REST GetOHLCData daily is the only correct source.

The 1H HTF (Gate 4) uses the ohlc(60) WS stream.
The 5-minute SSS (Gate 5) uses the ohlc(5) WS stream.
These are three separate data sources. Never conflate.

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-006  Timing
------------------------------------------------------------

Regime is computed ONCE per day at 00:00 UTC for every
monitored pair. Computation is triggered by the Regime
Engine task at 00:00 UTC (not by a candle close event).

  Computation trigger: 00:00 UTC daily
  Source: REST GetOHLCData (one call per pair)
  Update frequency: daily (regime does not change intra-day)

After 00:00 UTC computation, regime state is written to
the pre-computation cache for that pair. Pipeline Gate 3
reads the cached regime state — it does NOT call REST.
REST is called only once per pair per day.

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-007  response[-1] EXCLUSION — CRITICAL (GAP-03, AR-017)
------------------------------------------------------------

GetOHLCData ALWAYS returns the current not-yet-committed
candle as the LAST entry in its response array. This entry
MUST be excluded from ALL calculations.

  Correct:   response[:-1]  (all entries except last)
  Incorrect: response        (includes uncommitted candle)

The uncommitted last candle has:
  - A close price that is the CURRENT live mid-price
  - Volume that is partial (not the completed daily volume)
  - ADX, ATR, EMA values that would be computed from
    an incomplete candle and are meaningless

Including response[-1] corrupts all regime indicators
and produces incorrect regime classification. This is a
silent defect — no error is thrown. The computation
succeeds but produces wrong results.

THIS IS A CODING REQUIREMENT, NOT AN OPTION. Every
GetOHLCData response used for regime computation MUST
apply response[:-1] exclusion before any computation.

============================================================
6. COMPUTATION SPECIFICATIONS
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-008  ADX(14) Computation
------------------------------------------------------------

  Period: 14
  Algorithm: Wilder's DMI/ADX (standard Wilder method)
  Data: daily close, high, low from response[:-1]

  Step 1: True Range (TR) per candle:
    TR = max(high - low,
             abs(high - prev_close),
             abs(low - prev_close))

  Step 2: Directional Movement:
    +DM = max(high - prev_high, 0) if > (prev_low - low), else 0
    -DM = max(prev_low - low, 0) if > (high - prev_high), else 0

  Step 3: Wilder SMMA of TR, +DM, -DM (14-period):
    Seed: sum(first 14 TR) / 14, sum(first 14 +DM) / 14,
          sum(first 14 -DM) / 14
    Incremental: smoothed = (prev_smoothed * 13 + current) / 14

  Step 4: Directional Indicators:
    +DI(14) = 100 * smoothed_+DM / smoothed_TR
    -DI(14) = 100 * smoothed_-DM / smoothed_TR

  Step 5: DX per candle:
    DX = 100 * abs(+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI)

  Step 6: ADX = Wilder SMMA of DX (14-period):
    Seed: sum(first 14 DX) / 14
    Incremental: ADX = (prev_ADX * 13 + current_DX) / 14

  Minimum candles required: 28 (14 for DX seed + 14 for ADX)
  GetOHLCData returns 720 committed candles — adequate.

  State stored per symbol (regime):
    atr_14_daily:     Decimal  (daily ATR — used for vol state)
    adx_14:           Decimal  (current ADX value)
    plus_di:          Decimal  (current +DI value)
    minus_di:         Decimal  (current -DI value)
    smoothed_tr:      Decimal  (Wilder SMMA state)
    smoothed_plus_dm: Decimal  (Wilder SMMA state)
    smoothed_minus_dm:Decimal  (Wilder SMMA state)
    adx_smma:         Decimal  (Wilder SMMA state for ADX)

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-009  Daily EMA(20) and EMA(50) Computation
------------------------------------------------------------

  Periods: 20 and 50
  Algorithm: Standard EMA
  Alpha: EMA(20) = 2/21 ≈ 0.09524
         EMA(50) = 2/51 ≈ 0.03922
  Data: daily close from response[:-1]

  Seeding:
    ema_20_daily = sum(close[:20]) / 20  (SMA seed)
    ema_50_daily = sum(close[:50]) / 50  (SMA seed)

  Incremental:
    ema_20_daily = (close - prev_ema_20) * (2/21) + prev_ema_20
    ema_50_daily = (close - prev_ema_50) * (2/51) + prev_ema_50

  All arithmetic: Python Decimal. No float permitted.

  State stored per symbol (regime):
    ema_20_daily: Decimal
    ema_50_daily: Decimal

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-010  ATR(14) Daily Computation and Percentile Rank
------------------------------------------------------------

  ATR computation (daily Wilder SMMA — same as RE-008 Step 3):
    Seed: sum(first 14 TR) / 14
    Incremental: atr_14 = (prev_atr_14 * 13 + TR) / 14

  ATR percentile rank (50-day rolling window):
    Maintain a rolling buffer of the last 50 daily ATR values.
    At each daily update, push current ATR into buffer,
    pop oldest ATR out.
    percentile_rank = (number of ATR values in buffer
                       that are LESS THAN current ATR)
                      / len(buffer) * 100

  Parameters:
    atr_rolling_window: 50 (days)
    atr_percentile_thresh: 67th percentile

  Minimum candles required: 14 (ATR) + 50 (buffer fill)
  GetOHLCData returns 720 committed candles — adequate.

  State stored per symbol (regime):
    atr_14_daily:       Decimal  (current ATR value)
    atr_buffer:         list of Decimal (rolling 50 values)
    atr_percentile_now: Decimal  (current percentile rank)

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-011  Directional State Determination
------------------------------------------------------------

  Given ADX(14), EMA(20) daily, EMA(50) daily:

  if ADX(14) > adx_threshold (25):
      if EMA(20) > EMA(50):
          directional_state = TRENDING_POSITIVE
      else:
          directional_state = TRENDING_NEGATIVE
  else:
      directional_state = NON_DIRECTIONAL

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-012  Volatility State Determination
------------------------------------------------------------

  Given ATR(14) percentile_rank:

  if percentile_rank <= atr_percentile_thresh (67):
      volatility_state = NORMAL_VOL
  else:
      volatility_state = ELEVATED_VOL

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-013  Regime Assignment
------------------------------------------------------------

  asset_regime = f"{directional_state}_{volatility_state}"

  Valid regime strings:
    TRENDING_POSITIVE_NORMAL_VOL
    TRENDING_POSITIVE_ELEVATED_VOL
    NON_DIRECTIONAL_NORMAL_VOL
    NON_DIRECTIONAL_ELEVATED_VOL
    TRENDING_NEGATIVE_NORMAL_VOL
    TRENDING_NEGATIVE_ELEVATED_VOL

  Regime is stored in the pre-computation cache (I-2) for
  use by pipeline Gates 3 and 6 at each candle evaluation.

============================================================
7. TWO-LEVEL TRADE TAGGING
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-014  asset_regime and market_regime
------------------------------------------------------------

Every closed trade is tagged with two regime labels.
Both are written to the Logger Trade Outcome Bus on
every position close.

  asset_regime:  regime of the pair being traded at
                 entry time. Computed from the pair's
                 own daily candle data.

  market_regime: regime of BTC/USD at entry time.
                 BTC/USD is the market-wide context proxy.
                 Computed from BTC/USD daily candle data.

These two tags give CIATS the ability to segment trade
outcomes by both the individual pair's regime and the
broader market context simultaneously. Per-regime P&L
analysis (at 600+ trades, 100+ trades per bucket) requires
both dimensions to be available on every trade record.

------------------------------------------------------------
RE-015  BTC/USD as Market Regime Proxy (AR-074)
------------------------------------------------------------

BTC/USD is always in the monitored universe regardless of
volume ranking (0711003 AS-001). Its regime is computed
the same way as all other pairs — daily at 00:00 UTC.

BTC/USD regime is written to the pre-computation cache
as market_regime at the start of each day. All other pairs
read BTC/USD market_regime from the cache at entry time.

No additional computation is required for market_regime —
BTC/USD is already computed as a monitored pair.

============================================================
8. 1H HTF CONFIRMATION — GATE 4 (AR-053)
============================================================

Gate 4 is specified in AA=08 (Signal_Pipeline_Specification
0511002) and implements the 1H EMA alignment check. This
document records the strategy rule that Gate 4 implements.

  Rule: For TRENDING_POSITIVE pairs, the 1H EMA(20) must
  be above the 1H EMA(50) for entry to proceed to Gate 5.

  Data source: ohlc(60) WS stream (not REST).
               Pre-computation cache (I-2) stores the
               1H EMA values, seeded from ohlc(60) stream.

  Gate 4 applies to TRENDING_POSITIVE pairs only.
  NON_DIRECTIONAL + NORMAL_VOL pairs skip Gate 4.

  This 1H EMA check is separate from the daily EMA used
  for directional state. Do not conflate:
    Daily EMA(20/50) → directional state computation
    1H EMA(20/50)    → Gate 4 HTF confirmation

  Short-side 1H confirmation: Short_Module dispatch path
  for TRENDING_NEGATIVE pairs uses the Selection Controller
  short-side gate variant (AR-072). The short-side 1H
  confirmation logic is specified in 0811002 Selection_
  Controller_Specification and 0811005 Short_Module_
  Specification, not in this document. This section
  documents the long-side Gate 4 rule only.

9. HARD RULES — REGIME TAXONOMY
============================================================

HR-REGIME-001  response[-1] MUST be excluded from ALL
               GetOHLCData responses used for regime
               computation. No exception. No condition
               under which the last entry is used.
               (GAP-03, AR-017)

HR-REGIME-002  Regime is computed once per day at 00:00 UTC.
               Regime does NOT change intra-day. No
               computation is triggered by ohlc(5) events.

HR-REGIME-003  Daily candles are obtained from REST
               GetOHLCData only. The WS ohlc channel does
               not provide daily intervals. Never attempt
               to compute daily regime from WS ohlc.

HR-REGIME-004  One REST GetOHLCData call per pair per day.
               Not one call per gate evaluation. Regime
               state is cached and re-used all day.
               REST is called once at 00:00 UTC.

HR-REGIME-005  BTC/USD regime is computed every day.
               BTC/USD cannot be removed from computation.
               market_regime on every trade requires
               BTC/USD to be available. (AR-074)

HR-REGIME-006  All regime arithmetic MUST use Python
               Decimal. float arithmetic is PROHIBITED.

HR-REGIME-007  TRENDING_NEGATIVE applies a directional
               cascade at Gate 3 regardless of volatility
               state:
                 - LONG entry BLOCKED unconditionally
                   (entering long in a confirmed downtrend
                   is directionally incorrect).
                 - SHORT entry PERMITTED via Short_Module
                   dispatch.
                     AR-009 amended dv1_41: short via
                       Kraken margin trading per DEC-011
                       Stage M ratification.
                     AR-072: short SC-GATE-1 variant —
                       Selection Controller short-side
                       gate set.
                     AR-073: per-side state management +
                       pair_cache margin_eligible_flag
                       (population from REST
                       GetAssetPairs).
               No exception within the directional
               cascade. The cascade applies to BOTH
               TRENDING_NEGATIVE_NORMAL_VOL (Regime 5)
               AND TRENDING_NEGATIVE_ELEVATED_VOL
               (Regime 6).

HR-REGIME-008  NON_DIR + ELEVATED_VOL blocks entry
               unconditionally at Gate 3. No exception.
               Both directions blocked.

HR-REGIME-009  Regime thresholds are CIATS-owned starting
               values. No threshold may be changed by CIATS
               below the 200-trade floor.

HR-REGIME-010  Both asset_regime and market_regime MUST be
               written to every trade log record. Partial
               tagging is a defect. CIATS requires both.

10. CIATS PARAMETER REGISTRY — REGIME PARAMETERS
============================================================

All regime parameters are CIATS-owned starting values.
All will be evaluated and updated after the 200-trade floor
(per-regime analysis valid at 600+ trades).

  adx_threshold:          25    (trending vs non-directional)
  htf_ema_short:          20    (daily EMA short period)
  htf_ema_long:           50    (daily EMA long period)
  atr_percentile_thresh:  67th  (normal vs elevated vol)
  atr_rolling_window:     50    (days for percentile rank)

Evidence base for starting values:
  ADX(25) as trend threshold: 5+ independent sources.
  EMA(20/50) daily: standard trend identification pair,
  5+ independent sources. ATR 67th percentile for elevated
  volatility: 5+ independent sources. 50-day window for
  percentile: FP/DP analysis (TB00013-TB00014).

Break-even win rate for 1:1.5 R:R = 40%. Regime filtering
exists to ensure TothBot enters conditions with >40% win
rate expectancy. These starting values are derived from
that baseline.

Per-module CIATS framework (TB00000 v2_50 §9.6): Long
and Short modules maintain independent CIATS instances
with isolated statistical pools. Regime parameters
above are shared (regime classification is direction-
independent); CIATS may propose direction-specific
threshold adjustments at the 200-trade floor per
module-isolated statistical inference.

11. WHAT DOES THIS COMPONENT DO?
============================================================

(Five coding-readiness questions per TB00000 Section 3.2)

  (1) What does Regime Engine DO? (inputs → outputs)
      Inputs: daily OHLC candles from GetOHLCData
      (response[:-1]), for every monitored pair.
      Outputs: asset_regime string (one of 6 values),
      stored in pre-computation cache. market_regime
      from BTC/USD computation, also in cache.

  (2) What are its interfaces to other components?
      Reads from: REST GetOHLCData (0411002), monitored
      pair list (0711003), Parameter Store snapshot.
      Writes to: pre-computation cache (I-2) read by
      Gates 3 and 6 in pipeline. Trade Outcome Bus via
      Logger (asset_regime + market_regime on close).

  (3) What are its hard rules?
      HR-REGIME-001 through HR-REGIME-010 above.

  (4) What triggers it and what does it trigger?
      Triggered by: 00:00 UTC daily timer (not pipeline).
      Triggers: updated pre-computation cache for all
      monitored pairs, effective for all pipeline
      evaluations for the rest of the day.

  (5) What does it log?
      Regime computation completion (info). Any pair
      where computation fails (error + alert). BTC/USD
      market_regime value (info daily). Both asset_regime
      and market_regime tagged on every trade close.
