============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the complete specification for TothBot V2
asset selection: which Kraken spot pairs TothBot monitors,
how the monitored universe is populated at startup, how it
is updated in real time, and how BTC/USD is anchored as a
permanent member.

This document is the sole authoritative specification for
the monitored pair universe. It is a direct coding contract.
Claude must be able to implement the Selection Controller
universe logic from this document without asking any
questions.

3. MONITORED PAIR UNIVERSE — DEFINITION
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-001  Universe Definition
------------------------------------------------------------

The monitored pair universe is the set of Kraken spot
pairs that TothBot evaluates on every 5-minute OHLC candle
close. A pair must satisfy ALL three criteria to qualify:

  Criterion 1 — Quote Currency:
    Quote currency must be USD or USDC.
    Reason: USD pairs have the highest liquidity on Kraken
    (BTC/USD ~$196M daily volume at validation, TB00014
    Search 8). USD/USDC standardizes the quote currency
    for sizing computations.

  Criterion 2 — Instrument Status:
    Instrument status must be ONLINE.
    Determined from the Kraken instrument channel cache
    (see AS-004). Pairs with status post_only, cancel_only,
    limit_only, or delisted are excluded.
    Reason: non-online status means the pair is not
    available for full order execution.

  Criterion 3 — Minimum Volume:
    24h volume in USD >= min_volume_usd_daily ($500k starting).
    Reason: pairs below $500k daily volume have insufficient
    liquidity for reliable limit order execution at the
    position sizes TothBot uses. Thin pairs increase
    slippage risk and execution failure rate.

  Parameters (CIATS-owned starting values):
    min_volume_usd_daily: $500,000 USD

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-002  Universe Size — Top N by Volume
------------------------------------------------------------

From all qualifying pairs (meeting AS-001 criteria),
TothBot monitors the top N by 24h USD volume.

  Starting universe size: N = 50 pairs
  CIATS-owned parameter: monitored_universe_n = 50

  Selection rule: rank all qualifying pairs by 24h USD
  volume descending. Take the top 50. If a pair qualifies
  under AS-001 but does not rank in the top 50, it is not
  monitored. If a top-50 pair fails AS-001 criteria, the
  next qualifying pair by volume fills the slot.

  Rationale: Top 50 by volume gives a diverse but liquid
  universe without extending into illiquid territory.
  CIATS validates universe size after 200 trades and may
  adjust N via PDCA.

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-003  BTC/USD — Permanent Anchor (AR-074)
------------------------------------------------------------

BTC/USD is ALWAYS in the monitored universe regardless of
its volume ranking. BTC/USD cannot be removed by CIATS
parameter updates or universe rebalancing.

  Rule: BTC/USD is added to the monitored set before the
  top-N volume ranking is applied. The top-50 slots are
  filled from the remaining qualifying pairs after BTC/USD
  is guaranteed its position.

  Reason: BTC/USD is the market_regime proxy for all
  trades. Its regime must be computed daily (RE-015 in
  0711002). If BTC/USD fell outside the top 50 (unlikely
  given its volume) or had a transient status event,
  market_regime would be unavailable. BTC/USD is anchored
  to guarantee market_regime data continuity.

  BTC/USD also serves as the most liquid USD pair on
  Kraken and is a reliable CIATS reference benchmark for
  all regime analysis.

4. UNIVERSE POPULATION — STARTUP PROCEDURE
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-004  Startup Universe Population
------------------------------------------------------------

The monitored universe is populated at system startup
from the Kraken instrument channel WS subscription.

  Step 1: Subscribe to the instrument channel (WS v2
          public, no auth required) per 0411001.

  Step 2: Receive the initial instrument channel snapshot.
          This snapshot contains all Kraken spot pairs with
          their current status, price_increment, qty_min,
          cost_min, and 24h volume data.

  Step 3: Filter snapshot for AS-001 criteria:
          (a) Quote currency = USD or USDC
          (b) Status = online
          (c) 24h_volume_usd >= min_volume_usd_daily

  Step 4: Anchor BTC/USD (AS-003):
          Add BTC/USD to the universe unconditionally,
          regardless of whether it passed Step 3 filtering
          or would rank in the top 50.

  Step 5: Rank remaining qualifying pairs by 24h volume
          descending. Take top (N-1) pairs if BTC/USD was
          added, or top N if BTC/USD was already in the
          qualifying set.

  Step 6: Final monitored universe = BTC/USD union top N.
          Store universe as a set for O(1) membership tests.

  Step 7: For each pair in the monitored universe:
          (a) Cache instrument metadata: price_increment,
              qty_min, cost_min, status.
          (b) Subscribe to ohlc(5) and ohlc(60) WS streams.
          (c) Subscribe to ticker channel (for drawdown
              monitoring bbo — pairs with open positions).
          (d) Begin WARM_UP indicator seeding per 0711001
              SSS-009 through SSS-012.

Universe is not tradeable until indicator WARM_UP completes
for each pair (all five indicators seeded per HR-STRAT-007).

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-005  24h Volume Data Source
------------------------------------------------------------

24h volume is obtained from the instrument channel
snapshot at startup. The instrument channel provides
volume_base (base currency) and volume_quote (quote
currency, i.e., USD) for each pair in the snapshot.

Use volume_quote (USD denominated) for comparison against
min_volume_usd_daily. Do not use volume_base.

For USDC pairs where volume_quote is USDC-denominated:
treat USDC as equivalent to USD for filtering purposes.
No exchange rate conversion is applied (USDC ≈ $1.00).

5. UNIVERSE UPDATES — LIVE PROCEDURE
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-006  Instrument Channel Status Updates
------------------------------------------------------------

After startup, the instrument channel continues to fire
incremental status updates when any pair's status changes.

  On instrument channel status update:
    (a) If a pair's status changes FROM online TO anything:
        Remove pair from monitored universe.
        Unsubscribe from ohlc(5) and ohlc(60) streams.
        Log: PAIR_REMOVED reason=status_change new_status=X.
        If pair has an open position: do NOT remove until
        position is closed. Alert Bill.

    (b) If a pair's status changes TO online:
        Evaluate pair against AS-001 criteria (with current
        24h volume, checked against current Ticker or
        instrument data).
        If qualifies and universe size < N: add pair.
        Subscribe to ohlc streams. Begin WARM_UP.
        Log: PAIR_ADDED reason=status_restored.

    (c) If instrument data changes (price_increment,
        qty_min, cost_min): update metadata cache.
        Log: INSTRUMENT_METADATA_UPDATED pair=X.

Universe rebalancing (adding/removing pairs to maintain
top-N by volume) is a CIATS function, not a real-time
function. CIATS may propose universe changes via PDCA
after 200 trades. Real-time updates respond to status
events only.

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-007  Open Position Protection on Removal
------------------------------------------------------------

A pair is NEVER removed from the monitored universe while
TothBot holds an open position in it. If a status change
removes a pair from the universe while a position is open:

  (1) Block new entries for that pair immediately.
  (2) Allow the existing position to complete normally.
      Exit Controller continues monitoring and exits
      on its normal L1/L2 logic.
  (3) Alert Bill with the pair name and status.
  (4) Remove pair from universe after position closes.

============================================================
6. UNIVERSE ADJUSTMENT BY CIATS
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
AS-008  CIATS Universe Adjustment Procedure
------------------------------------------------------------

CIATS may propose changes to the monitored universe after
the 200-trade floor is reached via PDCA. Adjustable
parameters:

  - monitored_universe_n: expand or contract top-N size
  - min_volume_usd_daily: raise or lower volume filter

CIATS cannot remove BTC/USD. The anchor is permanent.

Universe adjustments are applied at inter-trade boundaries
only, consistent with all CIATS parameter updates. No
universe change occurs mid-pipeline-evaluation.

The universe adjustment procedure:
  (1) CIATS proposes new N or new min_volume via PDCA.
  (2) Bill approves (or CIATS auto-applies per 0211001).
  (3) At next inter-trade boundary, rebuild universe:
      Re-apply AS-001 criteria with updated parameters.
      Re-rank and re-select top-N.
      Add/remove subscriptions as needed.
      Log: UNIVERSE_REBALANCED pairs_added=X pairs_removed=Y.

7. HARD RULES — ASSET SELECTION
============================================================

HR-AS-001  BTC/USD is always in the monitored universe.
           No CIATS parameter update, rebalancing, or
           status event removes BTC/USD from monitoring.
           It is a permanent anchor. (AR-074)

HR-AS-002  A pair with an open position is NEVER removed
           from the monitored universe. Removal is deferred
           until the position closes. (AS-007)

HR-AS-003  All three AS-001 criteria must be satisfied
           for a pair to qualify for monitoring. Any one
           failing criterion excludes the pair.

HR-AS-004  Universe membership check is O(1) (set lookup).
           The monitored universe is stored as a set.
           Never perform linear search through a list.

HR-AS-005  CIATS universe changes are applied at inter-
           trade boundaries only. No mid-pipeline universe
           changes are permitted.

HR-AS-006  volume_quote (USD) is used for volume filtering.
           volume_base is never used for universe criteria.

HR-AS-007  CIATS may not change the three qualifying
           criteria (AS-001) — only the threshold values
           (min_volume_usd_daily, monitored_universe_n).
           The criteria structure is an architectural
           decision, not a CIATS-owned parameter.

HR-AS-008  Universe changes (additions and removals) are
           logged with reason codes. Silent universe
           changes are a defect.

8. CIATS PARAMETER REGISTRY — ASSET SELECTION PARAMETERS
============================================================

All asset selection parameters are CIATS-owned starting
values subject to PDCA optimization after 200 trades.

  monitored_universe_n:   50      (top N pairs by volume)
  min_volume_usd_daily:   $500k   (minimum 24h USD volume)

Evidence base for starting values:
  $500k USD daily volume: ensures adequate liquidity for
  TothBot position sizes (tradeable_pct=50%, per_trade_pct
  =5% of portfolio). At any reasonable portfolio size,
  5% of tradeable capital is well under 1% of daily volume.
  (FP/DP analysis — TB00020 Search 4, 5+ sources.)

  Top 50 by volume: diverse universe covering all major
  Kraken spot USD pairs with meaningful liquidity while
  excluding illiquid long-tail pairs. CIATS validates
  whether 50 is optimal after 200 trades.

9. WHAT DOES THIS COMPONENT DO?
============================================================

(Five coding-readiness questions per TB00000 Section 3.2)

  (1) What does asset selection DO? (inputs → outputs)
      Inputs: Kraken instrument channel snapshot and
      status updates. Parameter Store (min_volume_usd_daily,
      monitored_universe_n).
      Outputs: monitored_universe (set of pair strings),
      per-pair instrument metadata cache (price_increment,
      qty_min, cost_min, status).

  (2) What are its interfaces to other components?
      Reads from: WS Manager instrument channel cache
      (0411001, 0511001). Parameter Store snapshot (I-4).
      Writes to: WS Manager subscription list (new pairs
      added trigger subscribe actions). Pipeline Gate 2
      (Liquidity Gate) reads monitored_universe. Regime
      Engine reads monitored_universe for daily computation.
      Logger records all adds, removes, rebalances.

  (3) What are its hard rules?
      HR-AS-001 through HR-AS-008 above.

  (4) What triggers it and what does it trigger?
      Startup trigger: system startup → instrument channel
      snapshot → universe built → subscriptions initiated.
      Runtime trigger: instrument channel status update
      → evaluate add/remove per AS-006.
      CIATS trigger: PDCA proposal at inter-trade boundary
      → universe rebalanced per AS-008.

  (5) What does it log?
      At startup: UNIVERSE_BUILT pairs=N (list of pairs).
      On addition: PAIR_ADDED pair=X reason=Y.
      On removal: PAIR_REMOVED pair=X reason=Y new_status=Z.
      On deferral: PAIR_REMOVAL_DEFERRED pair=X reason=open_position.
      On CIATS rebalance: UNIVERSE_REBALANCED pairs_added=X
      pairs_removed=Y.
