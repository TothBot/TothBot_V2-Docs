3. SUB-DOMAIN 1 SCOPE
============================================================

Sub-Domain 1 (Strategy_Signals) governs:

  - The direction-symmetric SSS (Simple Signal System)
    signal model (consumed by both Long_Module and
    Short_Module per the parallel-module framework —
    TB00000 v2_50 §9.3 + 0500000 dv1_80 D7 Dispatch Seam)
  - The 6-regime taxonomy and all classification rules
    (including HR-REGIME-007 directional cascade per
    0711002 dv1_1 — LONG blocked + SHORT permitted via
    mod:Short_Module dispatch in TRENDING_NEGATIVE
    regimes 5 + 6)
  - The monitored asset universe and selection criteria

Sub-Domain 1 does NOT govern:

  - Selection Controller gate execution (AA=08)
  - Position sizing or R:R computation (AA=06)
  - Order dispatch or execution mechanics (AA=08)
  - CIATS improvement procedures (AA=02)
