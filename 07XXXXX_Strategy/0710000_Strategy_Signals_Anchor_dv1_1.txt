============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Sub-Domain Anchor for AA=07 Sub-Domain
1 — Strategy_Signals. It anchors the three instance
documents that specify TothBot V2 strategy signals, regime
classification, and asset selection to the Strategy Domain
Document (0700000).

This document defines the governance chain, the scope
boundary of Sub-Domain 1, and the mandatory reading and
dependency order for all instance documents in this
sub-domain.

============================================================
2. GOVERNING DOCUMENTS
============================================================

  0700000  Strategy          (Domain Document — parent)
  0311001  Namespace_Specification
  0311002  Document_Creation_Workflow
  0500000  System_Architecture_Overview (primary source)

============================================================
3. SUB-DOMAIN 1 SCOPE
============================================================

Sub-Domain 1 (Strategy_Signals) governs:

  - The SSS (Simple Signal System) signal model
  - The 6-regime taxonomy and all classification rules
  - The monitored asset universe and selection criteria

Sub-Domain 1 does NOT govern:

  - Selection Controller gate execution (AA=08)
  - Position sizing or R:R computation (AA=06)
  - Order dispatch or execution mechanics (AA=08)
  - CIATS improvement procedures (AA=02)
