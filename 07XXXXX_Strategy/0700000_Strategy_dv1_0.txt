STRATEGY
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Domain Document for AA=07 Strategy.
It defines the entire Strategy domain: its purpose, scope,
governing principles, hard rules, and the complete
structure of all controlled documents within it.

The Strategy domain governs every rule, model, threshold,
and decision that determines WHICH assets TothBot V2
monitors, WHEN a trade signal is generated, and WHAT
market regime conditions allow or block entry.

Nothing that touches signal generation, regime
classification, or asset universe definition is
unspecified. Nothing is assumed. Everything is explicitly
defined in this domain or in the instance documents
beneath it.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

The Strategy domain owns:

  - The SSS (Simple Signal System) signal model: the
    complete three-factor momentum scoring model operating
    on 5-minute OHLC candles
  - All indicator definitions, computation formulas,
    seeding procedures, and incremental update rules
    for RSI(14), EMA(9), EMA(21), and Volume MA(20)
  - The regime taxonomy: the 6-regime classification
    grid based on daily ADX(14), EMA(20/50), and ATR(14)
    percentile rank
  - All regime computation rules including data source,
    timing, response[-1] exclusion, and entry blocking
    rules per regime
  - The asset selection universe: the criteria for which
    Kraken spot pairs TothBot monitors and trades
  - BTC/USD anchor status as the market_regime proxy
  - All CIATS-owned strategy parameters and their
    starting values

The Strategy domain does NOT own:

  - Selection Controller gate logic (AA=08):
    Gate 5 (SSS), Gate 6 (Regime Gate) use Strategy
    outputs. AA=08 owns the gate execution logic.
  - Position sizing or R:R formula (AA=06)
  - Order dispatch and entry mechanics (AA=08)
  - Exchange wire protocol (AA=04)
  - CIATS improvement cycle procedures (AA=02)
  - System component architecture (AA=05)

The boundary is signal and regime definition. The Strategy
domain produces the signal and regime state. The Trading
System domain (AA=08) consumes them. The domains are
independent specifications. Neither owns the other's
content.

============================================================
3. GOVERNING PRINCIPLES
============================================================

3.1 Loss Prevention Takes Priority Over Profit Generation

Every strategy rule is designed first to avoid entering
bad trades, second to capture good ones. Regime blocking
(Gate 3) exists to prevent entries in adverse market
conditions. The SSS thresholds exist to require confirmed
momentum before entry, not to maximize signal frequency.
When signal quality and signal frequency conflict, quality
wins.

3.2 All Strategy Parameters Are CIATS-Owned Starting Values

Every strategy parameter — RSI thresholds, EMA periods,
volume multiplier, ADX threshold, ATR percentile, universe
size — is a starting value derived from FP/DP analysis.
None are sacred. CIATS replaces every starting value with
data after the 200-trade floor is reached. PDCA governs
all changes. The only permanent strategy decision is the
three-factor SSS structure and the 6-regime grid topology.
The values inside them are CIATS property.

3.3 Signal Must Align Across Timeframes

The SSS signal is validated by multi-timeframe alignment:
  - Daily regime (Gate 3): trend direction and volatility
  - 1H HTF (Gate 4): 1H EMA(20) > EMA(50) confirmation
  - 5-minute SSS (Gate 5): entry timeframe signal

All three timeframes must be aligned before a trade is
generated. A strong 5-minute signal in a bad regime is
rejected. This is the core structural principle of the
strategy.

3.4 No Indicator Reimplementation Without Specification

Indicator formulas are exactly specified in this domain.
RSI uses Wilder's SMMA (AR-076) — NOT standard EMA. This
distinction produces 5-8% variance in RSI values and
would systematically mis-fire the SSS RSI gate if
implemented incorrectly. The specifications in this domain
are the coding contract. Deviations require an AR and a
document update before code changes.

3.5 The Asset Universe Is Dynamic, Not Static

The monitored pair set is populated at startup and updated
in real time from the Kraken instrument channel. CIATS
may expand or contract the universe after 200 trades.
BTC/USD is permanently anchored regardless of CIATS
decisions — it is the market_regime proxy for all trades.

============================================================
4. STRATEGY ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
============================================================

TothBot V2 strategy operates on three layers:

------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Asset Universe Layer (0711003)
------------------------------------------------------------

Determines WHICH pairs TothBot monitors. Populated at
startup from the Kraken instrument channel. Criteria:
  (1) Quote currency: USD or USDC
  (2) Instrument status: online
  (3) 24h volume: >= min_volume_usd_daily ($500k starting)

Starting universe size: top 50 by 24h USD volume.
BTC/USD always included regardless of volume ranking.
Universe is live: updated when instrument channel fires
status changes. CIATS may adjust universe after 200 trades.

------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Regime Classification Layer (0711002)
------------------------------------------------------------

Determines WHICH market conditions allow entry. Computed
daily at 00:00 UTC for every monitored pair via REST
GetOHLCData (daily interval). One call per pair per day.

Six regimes on a 2x3 grid:
  Directional state (rows):
    TRENDING_POSITIVE:  ADX(14) > 25 AND EMA(20) > EMA(50)
    TRENDING_NEGATIVE:  ADX(14) > 25 AND EMA(20) < EMA(50)
    NON_DIRECTIONAL:    ADX(14) <= 25

  Volatility state (columns):
    NORMAL_VOL:         ATR(14) percentile <= 67th
    ELEVATED_VOL:       ATR(14) percentile > 67th

Entry is permitted in two of six regimes:
  TRENDING_POS + NORMAL_VOL:   FULL position
  NON_DIR + NORMAL_VOL:        50% position (Gate 6 sizes)
All other regimes: Gate 3 blocks entry.

Every trade is tagged with both asset_regime and
market_regime (BTC/USD proxy) for CIATS analysis.

------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 SSS Signal Engine Layer (0711001)
------------------------------------------------------------

Determines WHEN to enter a monitored pair. Runs on every
5-minute OHLC candle close after Regime (Gate 3) and HTF
(Gate 4) pass. Three factors must all be true:

  SC-SSS-1: RSI(14) on 5-min > 50 AND < 70
  SC-SSS-2: EMA(9) > EMA(21) on 5-min
  SC-SSS-3: Volume > Volume_MA(20) x 1.0x

All three conditions are required simultaneously. A pass
on two of three is a fail. SSS output is a signal_params
dict logged to CIATS Trade Outcome Bus on every evaluation,
pass or fail.

============================================================
5. HARD RULES — STRATEGY DOMAIN
============================================================

HR-STRAT-001  Strategy signals are generated only on
              5-minute OHLC candle close events. Intra-
              candle evaluation is prohibited. The candle
              close is the system clock.

HR-STRAT-002  SSS requires ALL THREE conditions
              simultaneously. Two-of-three is a fail.
              Partial credit does not exist.

HR-STRAT-003  RSI(14) MUST use Wilder's SMMA (AR-076).
              Standard EMA (alpha=2/15) is PROHIBITED for
              RSI computation. EMA(9) and EMA(21) use
              standard EMA. Only RSI uses Wilder's SMMA.

HR-STRAT-004  GetOHLCData response[-1] MUST be excluded
              from ALL regime calculations (GAP-03, AR-017).
              The last entry is always the not-yet-committed
              current candle. Including it corrupts all
              regime indicators.

HR-STRAT-005  Regime is computed from daily candles only
              (GetOHLCData interval=1440 or equivalent).
              The 1H EMA (Gate 4) uses the ohlc(60) stream.
              The 5-minute SSS uses the ohlc(5) stream.
              These are three separate data sources.
              Never conflate timeframes.

HR-STRAT-006  BTC/USD is permanently in the monitored
              universe. It cannot be removed by CIATS.
              It is the market_regime proxy. Every trade
              carries market_regime from the BTC/USD
              regime computation at trade entry time.

HR-STRAT-007  WARM_UP to READY transition requires ALL
              FIVE indicators seeded per pair (ATR(14),
              RSI(14), EMA(9), EMA(21), Volume MA(20)).
              A pair is not tradeable until all five are
              seeded. ATR-only seeding is insufficient.

HR-STRAT-008  CIATS parameter changes require the 200-trade
              floor. No strategy parameter may be changed
              by CIATS below 200 closed trades. EWMA
              monitoring is active below 200 trades but
              does not drive strategy parameter changes.

HR-STRAT-009  Signal params are always logged, pass or
              fail. The signal_params dict is written to
              the Trade Outcome Bus on every SSS evaluation
              regardless of result. This is CIATS data.
              No evaluation is silent.

HR-STRAT-010  Strategy domain does not own the Selection
              Controller gate execution logic. 0711001
              defines what the SSS signal IS. 0811002
              defines the gate that evaluates it. They
              are separate specifications. Never combine
              signal definition with gate logic.

6. STRATEGY PARAMETER SUMMARY
============================================================

The following parameters are CIATS-owned starting values.
All are initial values derived from FP/DP analysis with
5+ independent sources. All will be replaced by data
after the 200-trade floor is reached.

  SSS Signal Parameters (0711001):
    rsi_entry_low:          50   (RSI > 50 = momentum on)
    rsi_entry_high:         70   (RSI < 70 = not overbought)
    sss_ema_short:          9    (5-min EMA short period)
    sss_ema_long:           21   (5-min EMA long period)
    volume_sss_threshold:   1.0x (volume multiplier on MA20)

  Regime Parameters (0711002):
    adx_threshold:          25   (trending vs non-directional)
    htf_ema_short:          20   (daily EMA short period)
    htf_ema_long:           50   (daily EMA long period)
    atr_percentile_thresh:  67th (normal vs elevated vol)
    atr_rolling_window:     50   (days for percentile rank)

  Asset Universe Parameters (0711003):
    monitored_universe_n:   50   (top N by 24h volume)
    min_volume_usd_daily:   $500k (minimum 24h volume filter)

The one non-CIATS strategy decision: three-factor SSS
structure and 6-regime grid topology. These are
architectural decisions, not tunable parameters.

============================================================
7. RELATIONSHIP TO TRADING PIPELINE
============================================================

The 8-gate trading pipeline (0500000 Section 3) operates
on every 5-minute OHLC candle close. Strategy owns the
content evaluated at three gates:

  Gate 1  State Machine     — system readiness
  Gate 2  Liquidity         — 24h volume check (0711003)
  Gate 3  Regime Pre-Filter — regime blocks (0711002)
  Gate 4  1H HTF            — 1H EMA alignment (0711002)
  Gate 5  SSS Signal Engine — 3-factor signal (0711001)
  Gate 6  Selection Ctrl    — quality gates (AA=08)
  Gate 7  Risk Guard        — capital check (AA=06)
  Gate 8  Position Sizer    — compute size (AA=06)

Strategy domain content is evaluated at Gates 2, 3, 4,
and 5. Strategy defines the rules. Trading System (AA=08)
and Risk (AA=06) execute them. The domains do not overlap.

------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Sub-Domain Descriptions
------------------------------------------------------------

0711001  SSS_Signal_Model_Specification
  Complete specification of the Simple Signal System.
  Covers three SSS conditions, all indicator formulas,
  RSI Wilder's SMMA formula (AR-076), EMA standard formula,
  Volume MA formula, seeding procedures for all five
  indicators (AR-068), WARM_UP to READY transition rule,
  incremental update rules, and signal_params dict output.

0711002  Regime_Taxonomy_Specification
  Complete specification of the 6-regime classification
  grid. Covers directional state computation (ADX(14) +
  EMA(20/50) daily), volatility state computation (ATR(14)
  percentile 50-day rolling), data source (REST
  GetOHLCData daily), timing (00:00 UTC daily), response
  [-1] exclusion requirement (GAP-03), entry blocking
  rules per regime, two-level trade tagging (asset_regime
  + market_regime), and BTC/USD proxy role.

0711003  Asset_Selection_Specification
  Complete specification of the monitored pair universe.
  Covers selection criteria (USD/USDC quote, online
  status, volume threshold), universe size (top 50),
  BTC/USD anchor rule (AR-074), startup population
  procedure (instrument channel snapshot), live update
  procedure (instrument channel status events), and
  CIATS universe adjustment procedure.

9. RELATIONSHIPS TO OTHER DOMAINS
============================================================

  AA=05 System_Architecture_Overview (0500000)
    0500000 Sections 3 (pipeline), 4 (regime taxonomy),
    and the AR entries AR-067 through AR-076 are the
    primary source of record for all decisions captured
    in this domain. 0500000 references; AA=07 specifies.

  AA=08 Trading_System
    The Selection Controller (0811002) evaluates SSS
    output at Gate 5. The Long Module (0811001) dispatches
    entries for SSS-passing pairs. Gate 3 (Regime Pre-
    Filter) and Gate 4 (1H HTF) in the pipeline consume
    regime state produced by this domain. AA=07 defines
    the signal and regime state. AA=08 consumes them.

  AA=04 Exchange_Interface
    GetOHLCData REST call (daily interval) is the regime
    data source (AR-017, 0411002). The ohlc(5) and
    ohlc(60) WS streams are the SSS and HTF data sources
    (0411001). Exchange Interface defines the wire
    protocol. Strategy defines what is computed from it.

  AA=06 Risk
    Gate 7 (Risk Guard) and Gate 8 (Position Sizer)
    receive pipeline flow only after Gates 3-5 pass.
    Strategy domain does not own or reference risk
    parameters. Risk does not own or reference signal
    parameters. One-way dependency: Risk receives flow
    from Strategy pipeline output.

  AA=02 Operations / CIATS (0211001)
    CIATS owns all strategy parameters in the Parameter
    Store (except structural decisions). CIATS evaluates
    signal quality metrics from the Trade Outcome Bus.
    Strategy defines the parameters. CIATS evolves their
    values through PDCA after the 200-trade floor.
