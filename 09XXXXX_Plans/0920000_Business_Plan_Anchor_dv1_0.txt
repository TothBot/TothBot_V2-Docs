BUSINESS PLAN ANCHOR
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Sub-Domain Anchor for Sub-Domain 2
(Business Plan) within AA=09 Plans. It anchors the Business
Plan governance family to the Plans Domain Document
(0900000) and governs the instance document in this
sub-domain.

============================================================
2. SUB-DOMAIN DEFINITION
============================================================

Sub-Domain 2 — Business Plan — contains the business case
for TothBot V2. It defines the capital model, performance
targets, paper trading thresholds, go-live decision
criteria, and ongoing operational success metrics.
