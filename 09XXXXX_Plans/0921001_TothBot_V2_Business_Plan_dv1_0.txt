TOTHBOT V2 BUSINESS PLAN
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document defines the business case for TothBot V2.
It records the capital model, performance targets, paper
trading thresholds, go-live decision criteria, and ongoing
operational success metrics.

All parameter values in this document are CIATS-owned
starting values. They will be validated, corrected, or
confirmed by real market data during paper trading.
The only value that never changes is net 1:1.5 R:R.

This is a living document. It is updated when capital
parameters, performance targets, or criteria change.

============================================================
2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Objective: Generate consistent, risk-managed profit
from automated cryptocurrency spot trading on Kraken.

Operator: Bill (sole operator, sole beneficiary).
Exchange: Kraken Spot.
Instrument: Cryptocurrency spot pairs (USDT/USDC quoted).
Trading style: Systematic, signal-driven, long-only.
               Short Module deferred.

Primary constraint: Net 1:1.5 R:R on every trade.
                    HARDCODED. CIATS does not own this.
                    Never changes. Sacred.

Operating principle: Maximize profit. Minimize loss.
                     Loss prevention takes priority over
                     profit generation at all times.

============================================================
3. CAPITAL MODEL
============================================================

3.1 Portfolio Definition

  Total portfolio: All capital allocated to TothBot V2.
  Tradeable capital: 50% of total portfolio (starting).
  Reserve (50%): Not deployed. Drawdown buffer.
                 Protects against catastrophic loss.

  tradeable_pct = 0.50 (CIATS-owned starting value)

3.2 Position Sizing

  Per-trade allocation: 5% of tradeable capital (starting).
  Max concurrent positions: 20 (starting).
  Maximum theoretical deployment: 100% of tradeable
    (20 positions x 5% each).

  per_trade_pct = 0.05 (CIATS-owned starting value)
  max_concurrent = 20 (CIATS-owned starting value)

3.3 Position Sizing Transition

  At 200 closed trades: Fixed 5% per trade replaced by
  Half-Kelly position sizing.

  Half-Kelly: K% = W - (1-W)/R, apply 50%.
    W = win rate (from Logger trade outcomes).
    R = average win / average loss ratio.
  This is the CIATS statistical inference floor.
  No position sizing changes before 200 closed trades.

3.4 Fee Model

  Maker fee: 0.16% (entry via limit/post_only).
  Taker fee: 0.26% (emergency exits only).
  Fee tier baseline. CIATS monitors volume-tier changes.
  All R:R calculations use ACTUAL fill price.
  Net 1:1.5 R:R = net of all fees and slippage.

============================================================
4. RISK MODEL
============================================================

4.1 Per-Trade Risk

  MAE threshold (Layer 2 exit): ATR(14) x 1.5x (starting).
  Emergency SL (Layer 3): ATR(14) x 3.0x (starting).

  Emergency SL fires ONLY on VPS/TothBot/internet failure.
  Must NEVER fire in normal operation.
  If emergency SL fires in normal operation: critical
  defect. Full investigation required before trading resumes.

  mae_mult = 1.5 (CIATS-owned starting value)
  emergency_sl_mult = 3.0 (CIATS-owned starting value)

4.2 Portfolio-Level Risk (Circuit Breakers)

  Session pause drawdown: 5% of total portfolio (starting).
    Action: Pause new entries for remainder of session.
    Existing positions managed to close normally.

  Full halt drawdown: 10% of total portfolio (starting).
    Action: Close all positions. Cancel all orders.
    No new trading until Bill manually reviews and restarts.

  session_pause_drawdown = 0.05 (CIATS-owned starting value)
  full_halt_drawdown = 0.10 (CIATS-owned starting value)

4.3 Concentration Limits

  Max single-asset exposure: 3 concurrent positions
    per symbol (starting).
  Max correlated exposure: governed by Regime Engine
    regime classification.

4.4 Risk Architecture Reference

  Governing documents: 0600000, 0611001, 0611002,
  0611003, 0611004.

============================================================
5. PERFORMANCE TARGETS
============================================================

5.1 Primary Target

  Positive risk-adjusted returns over rolling 90-day
  windows after all fees, with:
    - Sharpe Ratio > 1.0 (annualized, 90-day window)
    - Maximum drawdown < 10% of total portfolio
    - Net 1:1.5 R:R maintained on every trade

5.2 Secondary Targets

  - Win rate: FP/DP-derived baseline, regime-segmented.
    No fixed target — CIATS optimizes from data.

  - Average hold duration: Signal-driven. No fixed target.
    max_hold_candles = 24 (CIATS-owned starting value).

  - Order fill rate: Post_only limit entry. CIATS monitors
    rejection_rate_by_regime to optimize entry.

5.3 Statistical Validation Thresholds

  50 candle evals:  EWMA Monitor valid. Monitoring only.
                    Not for inference. Not for decisions.

  200 closed trades: HARD FLOOR. All statistical inference
                     valid. CIATS Proposal Engine activates.
                     Half-Kelly replaces fixed per_trade_pct.
                     All parameter change decisions start here.

  600 closed trades: Per-regime analysis valid.
                     100+ trades per regime bucket.
                     Full regime-segmented optimization.

5.4 Performance Review Cadence

  CIATS: Continuous. Every 50 candle evals (monitoring).
         Every closed trade (Trade Outcome Bus).
  PDCA cycles: Inter-trade boundaries only.
  Bill review: After circuit breaker events. After 200
               closed trades. After 600 closed trades.
               As needed by CIATS recommendations.

============================================================
6. PAPER TRADING BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

6.1 Purpose of Paper Trading

  Paper trading validates the complete TothBot V2 system
  against live Kraken market data before real capital is
  at risk. It is not optional. Real capital is not deployed
  until all go-live criteria are met.

6.2 Paper Trading Parameters

  All parameters: Identical to live trading starting values.
  Capital: Simulated (no real orders executed).
  Market data: Live Kraken WS v2 and REST feeds.
  Duration: Until go-live criteria are met (Section 7).

6.3 Governed by

  0211005 Paper_Trading_Operations_Procedure.

============================================================
7. GO-LIVE DECISION CRITERIA
============================================================

All criteria must be met. No exceptions. Bill approves
the go-live decision.

7.1 Quantitative Criteria (minimum thresholds)

  (a) 200 closed trades completed.
      Rationale: CIATS statistical inference floor. Below
      200 trades, no reliable inference is possible.

  (b) No critical defects outstanding.
      A critical defect is any condition that could result
      in: unauthorized orders, incorrect position sizing,
      failed emergency SL placement, failed circuit breaker
      execution, or silent failures (no alert, no log).

  (c) CIATS Proposal Engine has produced at least one
      actionable parameter recommendation.
      Rationale: Confirms CIATS is functioning correctly
      as the continuous improvement engine.

  (d) Half-Kelly position sizing is active and producing
      results consistent with FP/DP expectations.

7.2 System Validation Criteria

  (e) All three Exit Controller layers validated:
      Layer 1: Signal-based and TP exits confirmed firing
               correctly on known conditions.
      Layer 2: MAE threshold breach (ATR x 1.5x) confirmed
               firing correctly.
      Layer 3: Emergency SL confirmed to be on matching
               engine. Confirmed it has never fired in
               normal operation during paper trading.

  (f) Both drawdown circuit breakers validated:
      Session pause at 5% confirmed triggering correctly.
      Full halt at 10% confirmed triggering correctly.

  (g) All alert channels confirmed operational:
      alert@tothbot.com receiving all expected notifications.
      No silent failures observed.

  (h) Logger confirmed writing complete trade records.
      CIATS confirmed reading Logger output correctly.
      All CIATS data streams producing valid output.

7.3 Qualitative Criteria

  (i) Bill reviews paper trading results and is satisfied
      with system behavior, including:
      - Regime classification accuracy
      - Signal quality (confirmed by trade outcomes)
      - Exit behavior across all regimes
      - CIATS recommendations appear reasonable

7.4 Go-Live Capital

  Initial live deployment: Full capital model as defined
  in Section 3. No additional conservatism required beyond
  the built-in 50% tradeable capital reserve.
  Rationale: Paper trading validated the system at these
  parameters. No reason to under-deploy.

============================================================
8. ONGOING OPERATIONAL GOALS
============================================================

8.1 Near-Term (0-200 live trades)

  - System runs continuously. No manual intervention
    in normal operation.
  - CIATS monitoring active. No parameter changes yet
    (below statistical inference floor).
  - First parameter change decision at 200 live trades.

8.2 Mid-Term (200-600 live trades)

  - CIATS Proposal Engine active.
  - Half-Kelly in operation.
  - Parameter optimization cycles running.
  - First per-regime analysis possible after 600 trades.

8.3 Long-Term (600+ live trades)

  - Full regime-segmented optimization active.
  - CIATS runs autonomously. PDCA governs all changes.
  - Pathway 1 GitHub automation deployed (PL-003).
  - System approaches full autonomous operation.

8.4 Continuous Goal

  Every day: Maximize profit. Minimize loss.
             Loss prevention takes priority.
             This never changes.
