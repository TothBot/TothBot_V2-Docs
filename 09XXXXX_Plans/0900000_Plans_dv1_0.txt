PLANS
============================================================

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document is the Domain Document for AA=09 Plans.
It anchors the Plans domain in the TothBot V2 document
hierarchy. It establishes the domain, defines its scope,
and governs all documents within AA=09.

============================================================
2. DOMAIN DEFINITION
============================================================

AA=09 Plans contains the forward-looking planning
documents for TothBot V2. These documents define:

  - The complete development roadmap from current state
    to live trading deployment.

  - The business case, capital model, performance targets,
    and go-live criteria for TothBot V2.

Plans documents are not specifications. They do not
define system behavior. They define intent, sequence,
milestones, and success criteria.

============================================================
3. SCOPE
============================================================

AA=09 Plans governs two sub-domains:

  Sub-Domain 1 — Master Plan
    Defines the full development roadmap for TothBot V2.
    Phase sequencing, milestone definitions, dependencies,
    and completion criteria for each development phase
    from document creation through live trading.

  Sub-Domain 2 — Business Plan
    Defines the business case for TothBot V2. Capital
    allocation model, performance targets, paper trading
    thresholds, go-live criteria, and ongoing operational
    goals.

============================================================
4. DOMAIN RULES
============================================================

DR-0900-001  All sub-domain anchors and instance documents
             in AA=09 must reference this Domain Document
             as their root authority.

DR-0900-002  Plans documents do not define system behavior.
             Behavioral specifications belong in AA=04
             through AA=08. Plans documents reference those
             specifications — they do not duplicate them.

DR-0900-003  Plans documents are living documents. They
             are updated as milestones are completed and
             as the system moves through development phases.
             Every update increments the version.

DR-0900-004  Plans documents are governed by the same
             document control rules as all other TothBot V2
             controlled documents: 7-field header, version
             increment on every change, Bill approval
             required for v state.
